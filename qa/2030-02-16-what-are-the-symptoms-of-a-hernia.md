Potential Complications of a Hernia

Incarceration This occurs when a hernia becomes stuck and can’t be pushed back into place. Incarcerated hernias can become painful and serious without treatment.

Strangulation This occurs when an incarcerated hernia doesn’t receive blood. As a result, the tissue may die.

Obstruction If an incarcerated hernia occurs in the bowel, it may lead to an obstruction, which prevents the passage of food and gas.

The Takeaway

A hernia occurs when an organ or tissue pushes through a weakened layer of surrounding muscle or tissue (often in the groin or abdomen), which may or may not produce a visible bulge. While some hernias don't cause any symptoms, others can lead to a feeling of burning, pain, or pressure. If you think you have a hernia, talk to your doctor.
