# When Will Netflix Drop the 'Love Is Blind' Finale?

Warning: The following post contains spoilers for Love Is Blind on Netflix.

The ninth episode of Netflix's new reality dating show, Love Is Blind, left us with so many questions as we wait for the finale: namely, what the hell happens next in Damian and Giannina's wedding ceremony? (Because the trailers sure make it look like Giannina goes running down the road in her gown before falling in a tragic heap.)

We also want to know what happens to the rest of the couples who met and got engaged without ever seeing each other: Lauren and Cameron; Kelly and Kenny; Jessica and Mark; and Barnett and Amber. While some of the couples seem built to last (looking at you, Lauren and Cameron), others could very well be hurtling toward doom (see: Damian and Giannina).

Which brings us to the point of this story:

When the heck is the Love Is Blind finale dropping on Netflix?

Netflix rolled out Love Is Blind in three separate installments, because apparently it's fun to make people wait for the juicy reality TV they so desperately crave. The streaming giant released the first five episodes on Thursday, Feb. 13, and the next four episodes on Thursday, Feb. 20.

[Drop the Love Is](https://barrierwatch.bbsxyxy.com/blog/3670230572.html)

The release date for the Love Is Blind finale is Thursday, Feb. 27. It'll be available starting at 12:01 a.m. PST/3:01 a.m. EST, according to Decider.

Will we be setting our alarm for the middle of the night? Quite possibly.
