Explanation

Severe lower back pain before your period can be a normal experience for many women. This pain is often linked to menstrual disorders. Some women may have increased sensitivity to pain during their period, which can make the back pain feel worse.

Causes of Lower Back Pain:

Menstrual Disorders: Menstrual issues can cause pain in the lower back. This is due to changes in your body during your cycle.

Increased Pain Sensitivity: Some women feel more pain during their period. This means that normal aches can feel much worse.
