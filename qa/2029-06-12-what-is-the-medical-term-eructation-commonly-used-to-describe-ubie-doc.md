Explanation

[medical term eructation](https://pepenails.com/fxrn1e5n/)

Eructation, commonly known as belching, is the act of releasing gas from the stomach through the mouth. This is a normal bodily function that helps to relieve the stomach of excess gas.

How Eructation Works

Physiology: The process of eructation involves the movement of gas up from the stomach, passing through the esophagus, and exiting through the mouth. It is a natural way for the body to expel swallowed air or gas produced during digestion.

Causes of Eructation

Normal Function: In most cases, eructation is simply a result of swallowing air while eating or drinking.

Digestive Disorders: Sometimes, frequent belching can be a symptom of digestive issues such as dyspepsia or gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD).

Understanding eructation can help differentiate between normal bodily functions and potential digestive issues. If belching is excessive or accompanied by other symptoms, it may be helpful to consult with a healthcare provider to explore underlying causes.
