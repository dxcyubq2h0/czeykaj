Explanation

Penile pain can be caused by several less common issues. One such cause is a chronic pain syndrome, which can develop after certain procedures or injuries. This type of pain can be persistent and challenging to diagnose. Another possible cause is a condition that affects the nerves or blood vessels in the penis, leading to discomfort that might not have an easily identifiable source.

Causes

Chronic Pain Syndrome: This can occur after surgeries or injuries. It involves ongoing pain and may require special treatments.
