# You Can Actually Visit These 'Stranger Things' Filming Locations in Real Life

Stranger Things has been a phenomenon ever since its debut on Netflix in 2016. Fans have clamored for any bit of info they can get about the show, from the ages of the series' kid actors to where the show is filmed.

Stranger Things was originally titled "Montauk," and the show's creators, Matt and Ross Duffer, wanted to film in the Long Island area, but the duo told The Hollywood Reporter that the area was too cold to film in. They settled in the Atlanta area, which reminded them of their childhood in North Carolina, and it's a perfect stand-in for the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana.

Here's what we know about the Stranger Things filming locations, and how you can visit them.

Jackson, Georgia: Hawkins, Indiana.

Jackson is about an hour outside of Atlanta and the city's downtown area is used for filming scenes of Hawkins. Some buildings in the town were painted over in order to make it ready for the 1980s.

Emory University: Hawkins National Laboratory

The university is the site of the former Georgia Mental Health Institute, and on the show it's used as the Hawkins National Laboratory.

EUE Screen Gems Studios: Interior Shots

For interior shots of places like the Hawkins Lab interiors and the Wheelers' and Byers' homes, the cast and crew head over to Screen Gems Studios to film. Fun fact — Screen Gems has also been the filming location for movies like Black Panther and The Haunting of Hill House.

Patrick Henry High School: Hawkins Middle School

Hawkins Middle School used to be Patrick Henry High School before it closed, and both exterior and interior shots of the school in Stockbridge, Georgia are used.

Bellwood Quarry: Sattler Quarry

The quarry has been the site of many important Stranger Things moments — it's where the (fake) body of Will Byers was found and its where Eleven broke Troy's arm after he threatened Dustin and Mike.

Jackson Drug Company: Melvald's General Store

Winona Ryder's character Joyce worked at the store, and although you can visit the store at 4 2nd Street in Jackson, the old drug store closed in 2017.

Tiffany's Kitchen: Benny's Burgers

On the show, Benny's Burgers is the restaurant where Eleven went after she escaped Hawkins Lab, and Benny, the restaurant's owner clothed and fed her. He was later killed by agent Connie Frazier.

In real life, Benny's Burgers is actually Tiffany's Kitchen, and the country eatery is located at 7413 Lee Road Lithia Springs, GA, and they serve breakfast all day long.

Piggly Wiggly: Bradley's Big Guy

If you're from the South or Midwest, you've probably been to a Piggly Wiggly before. The supermarket on 506 Center St in Palmetto, GA was turned into Bradley's Big Guy for the show, and it's the site of one of the most famous Stranger Things scenes: Eleven takes her Eggo waffles from this store.

Smokin' Cues: The Hideaway

The local hangout was perfect for the show, and it's where Hopper questioned and then beat up David O'Bannon. You can visit the location at 112 North Berry Street, Stockbridge, GA.

Gwinnett Place Mall: Starcourt Mall

The new Starcourt Mall is featured in the season three trailer, and it got its own ad on Twitter. Both Steve Harrington and Robin work at the "Scoops Ahoy" in the mall's food court.
