Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS) typically covers urgent care visits, though costs and coverage details vary by plan. Most BCBS plans require a copay for urgent care services, which is usually lower than an emergency room copay but higher than a standard primary care visit. Coverage may also depend on whether the urgent care center is in-network or out-of-network, with in-network providers offering the lowest out-of-pocket costs. To confirm your exact benefits, review your plan documents or contact BCBS directly.
