Understanding GLP-1 Therapies

GLP-1 receptor agonists such as Wegovy and Zepbound are FDA-approved for chronic weight management. They work by:

Mimicking gut hormones that regulate appetite

Slowing stomach emptying

Improving blood sugar control

These benefits also reduce risks of hypertension, type 2 diabetes, and heart disease—conditions that impact Black women disproportionately.

Why Denials Happen

Insurance denials for GLP-1 therapies can stem from:

Step Therapy Requirements: Mandating trials of older medications (e.g., metformin) first

Formulary Exclusions: New drugs often start on higher "tier" or non-preferred lists

Medical Necessity Criteria: Insurers may insist on specific BMI thresholds or documented comorbidities

Systemic Bias: Studies show Black patients sometimes face higher denial rates for specialty drugs

Understanding these barriers is the first step toward overturning a "no."
