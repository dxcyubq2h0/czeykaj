What is Biohacking?

Biohacking is all about enhancing physical and mental performance by taking control of your own biology. It is a mix of genetics, biology, neuroscience, and nutrition, all of which are aimed at optimizing health.

Biohacking, also known as DIY biology (do it yourself) is all about self-experimentation and finding what works best for your body.

The term “biohacking” went mainstream in 2014 when a Silicon Valley engineer introduced a meal replacement drink designed to hack nutrition. But biohacking has come a long way since then, it’s no longer just about food substitutes, it now includes advanced supplements, mental hacks, technology and even genetic modifications.

Biohacking is already transforming fitness, sports, and everyday life. Athletes are using peptides and SARMs to build strength and recover faster, while fitness junkies track sleep, metabolism, and glucose levels to improve their performance. Some biohackers swear by brain boosting compounds like nootropics and intermittent fasting to boost energy and bodily function.

Different Types of Biohacking

When we ask, what is biohacking? The answer isn’t just simple and limited. Biohacking isn’t a one-size-fits-all approach, it’s a broad concept that includes health, nutrition, fitness, lifestyle and technology. Health Biohacking

Health biohacking is all about preventive health, improving longevity, and keeping your body running at its best. It supports your internal system to function better, stronger and for longer.

Cold Water Therapy: Many individuals especially athletes and biohackers swear by ice baths to reduce inflammation, speed up recovery, and boost metabolism.

Intermittent Fasting: Simple yet powerful method, as it helps with blood sugar control, promotes fat loss, supports brain health and can prevent chronic diseases like diabetes or heart disease.

Gut Health Optimization: Probiotics, prebiotics and fermented foods help keep your microbiome in check, which affects your digestion as well as mood.

IVF (In Vitro Fertilization): This method is a reproductive biohack that is helping individuals conceive by fertilizing an egg with sperm outside the body and implanting the embryo into the uterus. It’s highly effective for those facing infertility.

Nutrition Biohacking

The way you fuel your body, determines how well it performs. Nutrition biohacking goes beyond eating “healthy”, it’s about customizing your diet based on your genetics, metabolism, needs and goals.

Ketogenic Diet: Many biohackers use keto diet to train their bodies to burn fat as fuel instead of relying on carbs.

Dietary Supplements: Adaptogens like ashwagandha, nootropics or smart drugs like lion’s mane, and amino acids like L-carnitine help with energy, focus, and stress management.

Nutrient Tracking & Blood Testing: Instead of guessing, biohackers use blood tests and wearable tech to see exactly what nutrients, less or more, they need.

Lifestyle Biohacking

Not all biohacking is extreme and risky, some of the most effective and common hacks are small daily changes which can make a big impact over time.

Blue Light Blocking Glasses: They help regulate your sleep cycle by reducing blue light exposure from screens at night and also manage eye strain.

Sleep Trackers: Many biohackers optimize sleep by tracking their REM cycles and sleep duration to improve recovery and brain function.

Grounding (Earthing): Walking barefoot on natural surfaces like grass, water, sand or marble is believed to help reduce stress, improve sleep quality and heart health.
