What conditions can lead to excessive daytime sleepiness and how can it be managed?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Excessive daytime sleepiness (EDS) is often linked to underlying conditions such as sleep apnea, narcolepsy, restless legs syndrome, or poor sleep hygiene. Effective management depends on identifying the root cause and may include medical treatment, lifestyle adjustments, and consistent sleep routines.

Because EDS can stem from many overlapping causes, self-diagnosing is difficult—and ignoring it may worsen your health, safety, and quality of life. A fast, free symptom check can help you pinpoint likely causes and clarify your next steps in minutes.
