# Your Whole-Family's Fitness Training Plan for Disney Parks

DO YOU NEED to train for Disney? Of course not. But does attaining a solid baseline of physical fortitude unlock a higher level of what’s possible? Consider what Jeff Galloway, the longtime Training Director for Disney marathons told me: “The Disney experience can be absolutely wonderful for adults and kids; it’s truly the happiest place I’ve ever been—and all that said, with the right training, it can be even happier.”

During my family's visit to Disney, I amassed 57,393 steps during three park days. I pushed a double stroller with and without 75 pounds worth of kids inside. I carried a diaper bag and sometimes a writhing child. Yet because of our plan from Galloway, my family and I were ready for it all. During the 11-week plan, Galloway had us gradually ramp up our step counts in stages.

Here's how my family got fit for Disney (and yours can too).

Week 1

Tues/Thurs: 3,000 to 5,000 steps total daily for adults; quarter-mile walk for kids

Sat and/or Sun: 7,000 steps total for the weekend for each member of the whole family (stroller and backpacks for adults encouraged)

Tues/Thurs: 4,000 to 6,000 steps total daily for adults; half-mile walk for kids

Week 3

Tues/Thurs: 5,000 to 7,000 steps total daily for adults; 3/4-mile walk for kids
