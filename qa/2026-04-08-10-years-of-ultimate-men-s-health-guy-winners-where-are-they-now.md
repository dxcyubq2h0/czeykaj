# 10 Years of Ultimate Men’s Health Guy Winners: Where Are They Now?

TEN YEARS AGO, Men's Health decided to do something that the brand had never done before: Instead of letting an athlete, actor, musician, or fitness model appear on the cover of the magazine, that honor would go to a reader.

"Men's Health has always been about the readers and positive masculinity, and we wanted to celebrate that by highlighting a real guy," says Ben Court, current executive editor of Men's Health, who was involved in the selection process of the first Ultimate Men's Health Guy competition. "We were looking for guys who were strong, broadly defined both physically and mentally, who had overcome some kind of challenge or adversity, and who gave back to their communities."

That first Ultimate Men's Health Guy was Noah Galloway, a retired Army combat veteran who had built back his mind and body after suffering horrific injuries during the war in Iraq.

Galloway's 2014 win changed the entire trajectory of his life, but it also set standard for future Ultimate Men's Health Guy contests. Thousands of men have applied to hold the UMHG title. In ten years, seven winners, including one reader's choice winner, have reigned victorious.

As we gear up for announcement of the next Ultimate Men's Health Guy, and to celebrate the 10th anniversary of the competition, we checked in with all the prior winners.What's kind of amazing is that every single one of these guys—through continued work on themselves and their communities—could still hold the title today.

Here's what each of them has been up to since their win.

NOVEMBER 2014 WINNER

Noah Galloway

[Health Guy Winners Where](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

43, Birmingham, Alabama

SOCIALS: Instagram, TikTok

GALLOWAY IS RETIRED Army combat veteran who lost his left leg and left arm during Operation Iraqi Freedom. After winning the very first Ultimate Men’s Health Guy competition, he went on to finish third in Dancing With the Stars in 2015, published his memoir, Living With No Excuses, in 2016, and won the premier season of Fox’s American Grit that same year. He has since launched the No Excuses Charitable Fund, which has donated resources to multiple charities, including Homes for Our Troops, his local YMCA and this year is supporting Sojourn Counseling and Heroic Hearts Project. Galloway is also a F45 Training U.S. athlete and a father of four. “On this 10-year anniversary of the Ultimate Men’s Health Guy competition, I’m proud to say that I’m living my best life,” Galloway said in a Men’s Health Instagram Live. And within the last several years, Galloway said he is focusing on building strength in an entirely different area of life: his mental health.

Tim Boniface

[of Ultimate Men Health](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/blog/6922766119.html)

45, Lexington, Kentucky

[Men Health Guy](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/)

BONIFACE IS A firefighter and EMT who left his job in finance after the terrorist attacks on Sept. 11, 2001. He wanted to pursue a more fulfilling career of saving lives even if it meant taking a large cut to his salary. He made it his mission to help his fellow public servants develop healthier diet and nutrition habits using his Ultimate Men’s Health Guy platform. Today, Boniface is now a lieutenant at the same Lexington fire department he was when he appeared on our cover. “I also currently doing fitness and nutrition training for clients,” Boniface says. “I have a number of firefighters who have become clients both locally and from other areas. I continue to live by my own code and eat healthy and stay fit.” He’s married and he and his wife are raising three girls. His next goal: “I’m working on becoming a stand-up comedian,” he says. NOVEMBER 2015 READER'S CHOICE WINNER

Aydian Dowling

37, Colorado Springs, Colorado

DOWLING, IS THE first transgender man to appear on the cover of MH. Dowling has continued his own fitness journey while also focusing on his nonprofit 501(c)(3) Point of Pride, which has helped 20,000 people around the world, along with his app TRACE, which are both created in support of transgender individuals seeking community and wellness. Dowling posts regularly to his huge social media following and also hosts the Glued Together podcast alongside his wife Jenilee, where they discuss lifestyle challenges from the trans perspective. Since appearing on the cover of Men’s Health, he also became a father.

NOVEMBER 2016 WINNER

Jedidiah Ballard, D.O.

42, Augusta, Georgia

SOCIAL: Instagram

BALLARD IS AN emergency room doctor, an associate professor at the Medical College of Georgia, and a former Battalion Surgeon in the US Army Rangers. Since being on the cover of Men’s Health, Ballard won a 17-mile adventure race, finished the World’s Toughest Mudder, became an ambassador for Isopure, and even participated as a contestant on The Bachelorette. He’s a Men’s Health Advisor and frequent contributor on topics including stress, teamwork, and life from the front lines.

NOVEMBER 2017 WINNER

Michael Dubree

37, Newburg, Kentucky

[Guy Winners](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/)

DUBREE IS LUCKY to be alive. In 2010, he entered recovery to deal with drug and alcohol addiction. Shortly after entering a halfway house, he discovered the gym and developed a love for fitness and nutrition. Fast forward to 2017 when Dubree went from a homeless drug addict to a MH cover star after being unknowingly entered by his girlfriend at the time. He is currently the co-owner of New Leaf Tree Service LLC, a tree trimming and removal service company.

JANUARY/FEBRUARY 2023 WINNER

Corwyn Collier

42, Maple Heights, Ohio

ARMY VET COLLIER rebuilt his body post-IED explosion after spending nine months in the hospital, becoming the first wounded/partial-amputee vet to obtain a pro card with the International Federation of Bodybuilding and Fitness. He is currently a teacher and track coach at Maple Heights High School. “I’m finishing up my second masters in educational administration, so I can look to become an assistant principal,” Collier says. He continues to document his own health and wellness journey on social media, all while running his own coaching and weight-loss consulting program—Collier Elite—alongside wife Iisha. What’s next: “Just focused on seeing my sons graduate and go to college,” he says. And “after taking three years away from the men’s physique stage, I will be back this fall to compete again.”
