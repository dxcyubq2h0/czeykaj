# The New 'Westworld' Trailer Stars ... Marshawn Lynch?

HBO's Westworld will return for a third season this year, and its premiere date is set.

The season will feature new characters and new actors, including Marshawn Lynch, Aaron Paul, and Kid Cudi.

Creators Jonathan Nolan and Lisa Joy are also rumored to have signed on with HBO for seasons 4 and 5.

It's been a little while since we questioned the nature of our reality. The last time we saw Bernard and Dolores was back in June 2018 when Jonathan Nolan and Lisa Joy's HBO sci-fi thriller wrapped its timeline-jumbled second season.

Uneven as the show may have been (season 1 found Anthony Hopkins and Ed Harris operating at peak cat and mouse, only to retreated tired ground for the next go-around), no series in the last decade has been quite as bold as Westworld. And, thematically, Westworld is as bold as they come. The series ruminates more on choice, identity, theories of mind, and general gamification than your ungroomed freshman year Intro to Philosophy professor. And while Westworld's dialogue can often feel like a grad student's attempt at soliloquy, the series' concept has still managed to feel new and exciting—no small feat in a post-Matrix world.

So regardless of your take on Season 2, be ready for an even bigger Season 3. Here's everything we know so far.

Is Westworld coming back for Season 3?

Yes, yes, it's really happening. The release date is set for the ides of March (that's March 15), almost two years since Season 2 premiered. When the series first aired in 2016, the estimated budget for the pilot episode alone was $25 million. From the Season 3 trailer, it doesn't look like the series is sparing any expense for its latest installment.

[Westworld' Trailer Stars](https://980187.com/kuv672g00/)

Is there a trailer yet?

The first trailer for Season 3 dropped last year and proved to be just the sort of confused, tell-nothing fare reminiscent of that other Nolan brother, Christopher (Inception, Dunkirk, Interstellar).

The newest trailer, which dropped yesterday, suddenly helps make everything clear. Except not at all. Even the feature length trailer gives us little in the way of specific plot points. But we can still speculate.

Who's in the cast?

[Westworld' Trailer](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/)

Returning to the series are most of the characters we've come to love, fear, and distrust: Charlotte, (Tessa Thompson), Dolores (Evan Rachel Wood), Bernard (Jeffrey Wright), Maeve (Thandie Newton), and Stubbs (Luke Hemsworth). As to whether these characters are still actually these characters and not AI copies of some other consciousness only using these characters as physical vessels to act out their own destructive fantasies (i.e., Ford played by Anthony Hopkins), we can't yet confirm.

New to the cast are other talent, including Aaron Paul (Breaking Bad), Scott Mescudi (Kid Cudi) and Marshawn Lynch (Marshawn Lynch!).

What's the plot?

[New Westworld' Trailer Stars](https://3899241.com/2hogfix/)

While plot details are sparse, it's safe to assume much of Season 3 will take place outside the park. The first season stayed mostly in the titular "Westworld" with Season 2 acting as a kind of escape (or, more aptly, an infiltration into the outside). The second season revealed the outside world to be one of "plenty," of wealth and technology and, apparently, enough boredom to send scores of Joes into a theme park to enact violent fantasies on AI flesh.

The new trailer teases out a little more about this "outside" world, or "your world" as Dalores narrates. Who is she talking to? Seems like good old Jesse Pinkman who has found the Westworld-escaped Delores in her naked animatronics birthday suit. Delores tells Jesse (we aren’t yet sure of his name yet) that he was also put into a cage and controlled. She claims something has been watching Jesse and controlling his future.

Cut to a rich man in a kind of blimp and then another (or same?) rich man in a big botanical-garden-like lair. The contrast between Paul’s character, who seems to work some kind of blue-collar mechanical job and the man wearing white and sitting at a dining table suggests we’ve traded themes of AI consciousness for class consciousness. The robot uprising will also be a worker uprising. (So this is … Elysium?)

Enter William who is still playing the game, somehow. Then a WWII theme park. Then Bernard stumbling around a farm. Then a one-armed Delores. Then William proclaiming his purpose is to “save the fucking world,” and Charlotte saying it’s “five of us against an entire world of them.”

So what we can gather is this. Some evil corporate enterprise is threatening to do something stupid that will end the world and now suddenly all the vengeful AI Westworld hosts want to help save the world and Jesse Pinkman will play a part as Marxist Jon Connor to help the hosts kill people … in order to? Who knows?

We can’t wait.

How many more Westworld seasons are planned?

According to reports, creators Jonathan Nolan and Lisa Joy have apparently signed deals with HBO to extend the series to a fourth and even a fifth season. Nolan has hinted in the past that the opening season of Westworld was just the "tip of the iceberg." And what timeline is this iceberg in exactly?

We remain cautiously optimistic.
