Explanation

An enlarged upper abdomen can be a result of swollen lymph nodes in the belly area. These swollen nodes, also known as lymphadenopathy, can be caused by various conditions. Mesenteric lymph nodes in the abdomen may become noticeable due to infections or inflammatory diseases.

Here are some common causes:

Infections: Sometimes, infections can cause the lymph nodes to swell up. This can be due to viruses, bacteria, or other germs.

Inflammatory conditions: Certain diseases that cause inflammation in the body, like Crohn's disease, can lead to swollen lymph nodes.

Liver-related issues: Swelling around the liver, known as periportal lymphadenopathy, can also lead to an enlarged abdomen.

If you notice any unusual swelling in your abdomen, it is important to consult a healthcare professional for a proper diagnosis and treatment plan.
