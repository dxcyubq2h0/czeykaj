# How can I safely exercise with lower back pain? | Ubie Doctor's Note

How can I safely exercise with lower back pain?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Exercising safely with lower back pain starts with low-impact activities like walking, swimming, and gentle stretching. Begin slowly, focus on core-strengthening moves, and gradually increase intensity as your body tolerates it. Avoid heavy lifting or high-impact exercises during flare-ups, and stop if pain worsens.

Because lower back pain can stem from many causes—muscle strain, disc issues, posture, or something more serious—knowing the source is key to choosing safe exercises and avoiding setbacks. A free, instant, online symptom check can help you understand what may be driving your pain and guide your next steps, so you can move forward with confidence.
