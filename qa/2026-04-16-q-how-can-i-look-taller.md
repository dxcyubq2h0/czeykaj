[How Can Look](https://nootropic-atlas.escortskhi.com/)

The average height for the American male hovers around 5’ 9”, but most guys want you to believe they’re inches taller than that. Amongst all of the tricks out there that create optical illusions to add some height to your frame, here are our favorites to look as tall as you feel.

[How Can Look](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/)

Find the perfect fit. You mentioned that nothing fits correctly. That’s OK—buying off the rack is a no-go for a lot of men. Invest in a few wardrobe cornerstones (jeans, T-shirts, suits, dress shirts) and have them tailored to your proportions. Trust us, once you see the superior fit, you'll know why it's worth the investment. Along these lines, avoid bulky knits since they’ll overwhelm your frame. A perfectly tailored wardrobe will take you far.

[How Can Look](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/)

Wear patterns that work for you. Stripes can elongate your shape—if you stick with thin stripes that are proportioned to your frame. The same rule goes with checks and other patterns: Make sure they’re small or you run the risk of being swallowed by the pattern.

Avoid low-rise. Allow your pants to sit around your natural waistline since the low-rise will make your legs appear shorter. Just don’t pass the belly button, OK Urkel?

Go monochromatic. Dressing in one color will extend your silhouette. If you’re trying to add more interest to a solid suit, pinstripes also help stretch and elongate your body.

[How Can Look](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/blog/0657393996.html)

Keep it light. Fashion’s golden rule of wearing dark colors for a flattering look can be flipped upside down here. Choose light colors, like gray, to add more breadth to your appearance.
