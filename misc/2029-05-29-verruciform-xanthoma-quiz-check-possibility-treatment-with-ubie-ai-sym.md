What is Verruciform Xanthoma?

This is a rare, benign condition affecting the oral cavity's inner lining. It appears as a rough or pebbly-textured growth. The cause is unclear but may be associated with human papillomavirus (HPV). Both men and women are affected, and it is commonly seen in the 40 to 70-year-old age group.

Treatment of Verruciform Xanthoma

Treatment involves surgical removal of the lesion.
