Ellen Barkin

Most recently known for her longtime lead role in Animal Kingdom as the matriarch of a crime family, Ellen Barkin is a Tony and Emmy-award winning actress who's starred in various films and television shows such as Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas, Switch, and Before Women Had Wings. In "Exit Stage Death," she plays Kathleen Townsend, a former sitcom star who begs her old co-star to appear in a one-night only production for a chance to return to stardom.

After her appearance on the show, Barkin said she'd do it again without question in an interview with The Hollywood Reporter. "I just want to come back every year and play different people. You think anyone will want me to play like, a nice old lady? Who is sweet and understanding? I was going to try to make Kathleen a nice old lady because I was like, “Not again!” But, she has to be a mean old lady."
