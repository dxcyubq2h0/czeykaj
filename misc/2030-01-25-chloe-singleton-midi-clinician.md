Chloe Singleton, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

Built in women’s health: "I’ve spent my entire career in OB/GYN, beginning in mother-baby care and community health after earning my Bachelor of Science in Nursing in 2014. In 2022, I transitioned into a broader provider role in women’s healthcare, continuing my passion for caring for women through every stage of life. Over the years, I’ve seen how often women’s concerns, especially during perimenopause and menopause, can be overlooked or dismissed. That experience is what ultimately drew me to Midi, where I can focus on helping women feel heard, supported, and empowered in their care."

When not being heard becomes personal: " After delivering my daughter via cesarean section, I experienced severe complications that were repeatedly dismissed. Despite my clinical background, I wasn’t listened to. Weeks later, I passed retained placental tissue that had been missed, something that could have cost me my life. That experience changed me. It deepened my commitment to truly listening to my patients and trusting that they know their bodies."

Clarity in a confusing time: "What inspires me most is helping women make sense of symptoms that are often overlooked or misunderstood. I focus on creating space for patients to feel heard, validated, and supported, while offering clear, evidence-based solutions that improve their quality of life."

Care that centers you: "My approach is rooted in patient-centered, evidence-based care. I prioritize listening, education, and shared decision-making so every patient feels confident, informed, and in control of their health."
