What is Clomiphene Citrate?

Clomiphene citrate is a selective estrogen receptor modulator (SERM) commonly used to treat female infertility. In men, it acts by blocking estrogen receptors in the hypothalamus and pituitary gland, which are key areas of the brain involved in regulating hormone production.

By blocking these receptors, clomiphene citrate disrupts the negative feedback loop that normally suppresses the production of luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH). As a result, the body is stimulated to produce more of these hormones, which in turn leads to increased testosterone production in the testes.

This mechanism of action makes clomiphene citrate an effective therapeutic option for men with hypogonadism, a condition characterized by low testosterone levels. By boosting natural testosterone production, clomiphene citrate can help alleviate symptoms associated with low testosterone, such as fatigue, decreased muscle mass, and reduced libido.
