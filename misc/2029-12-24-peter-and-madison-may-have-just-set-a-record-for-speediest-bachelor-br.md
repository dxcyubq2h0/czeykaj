However, all of the season's dramatics seemed to be worth it when Peter revealed that he and runner-up Madison Prewett were now in a relationship during the "After the Final Rose" special. The couple genuinely seemed into each other, and if you forgot the fact that they had fundamental differences and their families didn't get along, it almost seemed like they could go the long run...lol jk most of us expected a breakup, but this one is fast even by Bachelor Nation standards.

Just two (!) days after announcing that they were in a relationship, Peter and Madison released separate statements announcing their break-up.

Peter's statement reads, in part, "Madi and I have mutually decided to not pursue our relationship any further. Believe me this was not easy for either of us to be ok with, but after a lot of honest conversations, we have agreed that this is what makes most sense for the two of us." It's accompanied by a photo of him looking pensive.

Former Bachelor and Bachelorette contestants flooded both posts with positive comments, and Peter even commented on Madison's post with a red heart, with the Alabama native responding with a heart of her own.
