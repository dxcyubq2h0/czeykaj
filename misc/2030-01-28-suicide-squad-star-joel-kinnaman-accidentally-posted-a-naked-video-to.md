It all went down while he was on location in Atlanta, filming the Suicide Squad sequel, and missing his home country of Sweden.

"Yeah, I struggle with this Instagram thing, you know?" He began. "I'm obsessed with the Swedish soccer team, I actually played in the team when I was younger and I follow all the games. So far, so innocent. Kinnaman carried on watching the game, his mood boosted, and before long his phone was blowing up.

"And I'm getting comments, so I feel a little connected," he says. "Then my girlfriend calls me, and it's a really intense part of the game, usually I'd answer but I let it slip to voicemail. And she calls again, and I'm like, 'it's nearly halftime, I'll call her at halftime'... And then she calls a third time. And there's something about that third call that kind of stuck out a little bit because she doesn't call me three times in a row. So I pick up and I'm like, 'Hey babe, you know, I'm watching the game.' And she's like, 'Yeah, I know.' I'm like, 'Oh yeah, you saw it on Instagram.'
