How does Peacemaker season 2 tie in to the new DCU and Superman?

The establishment of the DCU (DC Universe) has been called a 'soft reboot' from the DECU because while some characters are changing and their stories are starting over from scratch (like in Superman), others, like Peacemaker, are sticking around as is. We actually saw Peacemaker in Superman, appearing as a talking head guest and trashing Supes on the right-wing show The Sphere, hosted by Cleavis Thornwaite (Michael Ian Black). So we know he's firmly in that world. But how?

Peacemaker explains the universe change in a typically tongue-in-cheek way in the first episode of season 2. During the 'Previously on...' segment that opens the episode, we see much of what happened in the first season—Peacemaker living in guilt after the events of The Suicide Squad, his fraught relationship with his father, the flashback to his traumatic childhood, and the whole Butterfly Alien Invasion situation. But we also see some slightly-altered moments: Adebayo (Danielle Brooks) talks about calling in the Justice Gang instead of the the Justice League. And in the moment where season 1 actually featured cameos from the Justice League (and specifically Jason Momoa and Ezra Miller), we now see a re-filmed cameo from the Justice Gang (as introduced in Superman), with cameos specifically from Nathan Fillion's Green Lantern, Guy Gardner, and Isabela Merced's Hawkgirl. It's a fun, clever, and funny way to address the universe change—essentially just saying, well, deal with it. We're even told of a history of Guy and Peacemaker hanging out. Sure! That's one night out we'd love to observe as a fly on the wall.

The new DCU will continue to sink its teeth into season 2 of Peacemaker. The very first episode of season 1 includes cameos from Fillion, Merced, and Sean Gunn (as Maxwell Lord), all reprising characters they debuted in Superman, helping to further ground the new season in the new universe that James Gunn is putting in some seriously solid work in building out. Frank Grillo, who played Rick Flag. Sr. in both the animated series Creature Commandos and in Superman, plays a key role in Peacemaker season 2—hunting down Peacemaker and seeking vengeance for his son's death. More on that below.

What do I need to know or remember from Peacemaker season 1 before watching season 2?

We kind of got at it a bit above, but the crux of what you'll need to remember from Peacemaker season 1 is that Peacemaker used to be a cold-blooded killing machine without much of a moral compass. We saw this version of Peacemaker in the film The Suicide Squad, where he was more or less an assassin doing Amanda Waller's (Viola Davis) immoral bidding; He eventually murdered Rick Flag Jr. (Joel Kinnaman), who was very much a good guy.

Peacemaker season 1 finds its titular character wracked with guilt over that, realizing what he's done wrong—particularly killing Flag—and wanting to change his ways. We find out that Peacemaker is the way he is in large part due to his miserable father (who happens to be a White Supremacist supervillain named White Dragon, played by Robert Patrick) who psychologically tormented him as a kid, turning him and his brother into killing machines—eventually leading to his brother's death as a child.

While Peacemaker is going through all of this, though, he gets drafted onto Task Peacemaker, which becomes a typical band of James Gunn misfits; the team includes Peacemaker along with his psychotic friend Adrian Chase/Vigilante (Freddie Stroma), A.R.G.U.S. agents John Economos (Steve Agee) and Emilia Harcourt, along with Adebayo (Brooks), who also happens to be Waller's daughter, and Peacemaker's beloved Bald Eagle, Eagly. This crew eventually is tasked, off the books, with stopping an outrageous alien invasion from a bunch of evil body-snatching Butterfly aliens. It's wild and getting much more into it won't change much—but it's wild.

And, in short, that's all you need to know. Peacemaker becomes a much more sympathetic, tragic figure by the end of the season, becoming particularly close with Adebayo as well as everyone else on the time. He also gets closure at a certain point by shooting his terrible father in the head, though his spirit eventually appears to him—signifying that he won't be losing him as easy as he'd hoped.

Peacemaker season 2 promises a universe-jumping, world-expanding adventure, and so this world will only become bigger, wilder, and possibly harder to follow. But this is a story we're all-in on, and characters we love to spend time with—so we're along for the journey no matter where it ends up.
