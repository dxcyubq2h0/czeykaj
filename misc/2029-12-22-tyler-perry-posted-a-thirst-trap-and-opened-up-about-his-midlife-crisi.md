# Tyler Perry Posted a Thirst Trap and Opened Up About His 'Midlife Crisis'

As 2020 comes to a close, it’s natural for people to reflect on the end of a challenging year and focus on new beginnings. Judging from actor Tyler Perry’s most recent Instagram post, it's clear he's among those who are embracing the chance to do some major self-reflection—literally, in this case.

The Madea’s Family Reunion star took to his Instagram account on Wednesday to shed some light on what’s going on in his personal life, all while showing off his relatively svelte physique in a mirror selfie. Posing in workout gear, Perry revealed he not only split from long-time girlfriend Gelila Bekele, but that he’s struggling to figure out how to move forward going into 2021.

“This is what a mid-life crisis looks like,” the House of Payne producer captioned the photo. “I’m 51, single and wondering what the next chapter in my life will look like.”

Despite Perry's recent rough patch, however, he seems to be approaching his next phase of life with optimism.
