TO MAKE a lasting impression in black-tie affairs or formal occasions, tuxedo shoes are mandatory. Whether you're attending a gala, a wedding, a soirée, or walking the red carpet, the right tuxedo shoes should not only complete your elegant ensemble but also provide lasting comfort throughout the event.

When selecting these dress shoes, consider a few key factors. The best tuxedo shoes need to be crafted in premium materials, usually black patent leather, to warrant durability and look refined with a nice shine. On the other hand, comfort is non-negotiable, and the best tuxedo shoes should have well-cushioned insoles or footbeds and a secure fit to provide support, allowing you to dance the night away with ease. Lastly, tuxedo shoes will come in a variety of formal footwear styles, from classic lace-up Oxford and derby shoes to casual leather loafers or velvety slip-on shoes, so you can choose one that resonates with your personal taste while matching your tux and the dress code.

How to Choose the Best Tuxedo Shoes

Style: Available in many footwear styles from loafers to Oxfords, the best tuxedo shoes should align with the formality and dress code of the event and your personal style. Classic black patent leather shoes are a safe bet for most formal occasions, for instance. However, if you're aiming for a more casual or unique look, consider options like velvet loafers or leather slip-on shoes.

Comfort and fit: For when you'll be on your feet for an extended period in tuxedo shoes, make sure they come with cushioned insoles or adequate arch support. In addition, the best tuxedo shoes need to fit perfectly to prevent blisters and should ensure that there's enough room in the toe box. Width-wise, the shoes should hug your foot snug without pinching.

Outsole: In tuxedo shoes, leather soles are the epitome of elegance and are suitable for most formal occasions, but they’re not the most walking-friendly. So consider rubber-soled tuxedo shoes if you anticipate walking on slick surfaces or want added traction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What shoe styles are best for tuxedo suits?

Oxford shoes are the quintessential choice for pairing with tuxedo suits, especially those in plain toe and cap toe. Dress shoes in patent leather also work wonders with a tux: the leather has a high-gloss finish to reflect light and thus look polished, adding a touch of luxury and formality to your fits. Velvety slippers or slip-on shoes will look great with a casual tux while adding some nonchalant flair and are overall popular for less formal black-tie events. Lastly, while loafers are generally considered less formal than Oxfords, there are many styles of loafers, such as penny and Venetian loafers, that are suitable for certain tuxedo occasions.

Do tuxedo shoes have to be black?

Black is the traditional choice for tuxedo shoes. But unless the dress code absolutely demands black, your black-tie ensemble will look great with other deep shades like navy/midnight blue or rich.
