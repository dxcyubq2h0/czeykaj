# Strongman Martins Licis Shows Off His Weekly Competition Prep Workout Schedule

Former World's Strongest Man Martins Licis has had a heck of an end to 2021. After rehabbing extensive injuries (including being hit by a car) that forced him to sit out most competitions in 2020 and the first part of 2021, Licis bounced back with a vengeance and won Rogue’s first Strongman Invitational this October.

Now, he's prepping for his next big competition: the 2022 Arnold Strongman Classic, which is scheduled to place in March 2022. He's giving fans a look at what goes into a full week of training for the big show in a new video.

He starts out Day 1 of training Husafell carries, walking for 360 feet holding the awkward load of 200 pounds for multiple sets.

"I'm just getting my feet moving again... I'm just trying to consistently hit it and not to grind myself too much. There's time and space for that," says Licis. "Over the next couple months I'll be increasing that intensity and weight, trying to maintain that distance."

He notes that he loves doing Husafell carries because it's "not too intense on the knees, not too intense on the back, and it does put the body under load but I can go the distance and really get out of breath and feel a quad burn."

Next, he works on Atlas stone holds, hauling the massive boulder off the floor. His goal is to hold the stone and hover above the ground without squeezing it with his legs and shins.

Day 2 of training is back day, which includes farmer's carries and banded deadlifts, which he loads with 500 pounds of weight and 180 pounds of additional resistance from bands. He works through for five to 10 pulls of the heavy weight.

"Oh man, that's more tiring than I'd like it to be," says Licis. He goes on to recommend the specialized platform he uses for banded deadlifts for anyone looking to level-up their pulls—but cautions they're super tough.

Next, he hits pullups with three sets of 90-degree elbow holds.

[His Weekly](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Day 3 is log pressing day, including log strict presses to get his shoulders stronger, along with dumbbell shoulder presses on an incline bench.

[Prep Workout](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/blog/8032842362.html)

"I'm gonna be doing a log strict press up until I find out what the events are... I don't want to put too much effort doing a full-on log clean and press for every rep. I just really want to get my shoulders stronger and get ready for whatever is to come," says Licis.

For Day 4 (his final day in this video of this training) is leg day, which includes heavy yoke carries and banded squats.

"I want to keep practicing deep squats, but also just to take a little pressure off my knees so I'm not going to go as heavy... but I want to keep that top part heavier so I can keep the intensity," says Licis.
