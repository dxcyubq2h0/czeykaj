What is Lichen Nitidus / Lichen Striatus?

[Quiz Check](https://immunopeptide.xianfengchem.com/blog/1430670404.html)

This is a skin condition characterized by the presence of raised, pink bumps that appear in a linear pattern on the skin. While this condition is commonly observed in individuals of various age groups, its exact cause remains unknown.

Typical Symptoms of Lichen Nitidus / Lichen Striatus

Thick, leathery skin

Skin lumps

Smooth, shiny lump on the skin

Red or yellow skin lumps

Itchy skin

Redness in sun-exposed areas of skin

Small bumps on the skin, similar to goose bumps

Welts appear when skin is scratched or rubbed

Diagnostic Questions for Lichen Nitidus / Lichen Striatus

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have areas of rough or thick skin?

Have you noticed any lumps on your skin?

Is there a shiny or waxy, pearl-like lump on the skin?

Do you have yellow or red lumps/bumps on your skin?

Do you have itchy skin?

Treatment of Lichen Nitidus / Lichen Striatus

The rashes will go away after 6-12 months. This may leave light or dark marks, which usually will go away after some time too. There is no cure, but moisturizers and steroid creams may help with any itch or dry skin.
