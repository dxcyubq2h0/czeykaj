[Anemia Critical Bloodwork Signs](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/blog/8378931691.html)

Anemia is one of the most common signs of multiple myeloma, affecting up to 75% of patients at diagnosis. It typically appears as a low red blood cell count (low hemoglobin) along with visible changes in the shape and size of red blood cells on a blood smear. These abnormalities—often described as normocytic, normochromic anemia with rouleaux formation (red blood cells stacked like coins)—can be detected through a routine complete blood count (CBC) and peripheral blood smear.

Hemogram Insights

A hemogram is a detailed blood test that gives important information about blood cells. It can help identify anemia by showing low red blood cell counts and changes in their appearance.
