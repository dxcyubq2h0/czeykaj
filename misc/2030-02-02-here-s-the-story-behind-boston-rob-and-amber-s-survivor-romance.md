When did Boston Rob and Amber get married?

Rob and Amber got married in on April 16, 2005 at the Atlantis Paradise Island in the Bahamas. A month later, CBS aired a two-hour special about their wedding called Rob and Amber Get Married.

How many kids do Rob and Amber have?

The couple are the proud parents of four daughters: Lucia, Carina, Isabetta, and Adelina. The girls are also Survivor fans, so there's a possibility they could follow in their parents' footsteps and compete for the title of Sole Survivor.
