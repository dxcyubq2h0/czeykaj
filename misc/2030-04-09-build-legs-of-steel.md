# Build Legs of Steel!

The squat is one of the best lower-body exercises you can do. It burns more calories per rep than almost any other exercise, and it hits the majority of muscles south of your hips. So how do you make it even better? Try the rear-foot-elevated split squat without stopping—the main move in this challenge.

“Since you’re standing on one leg, the instability of the exercise calls more muscle into play as you try to stay balanced,” says BJ Gaddour, C.S.C.S., metabolic training expert and owner of StreamFit.com. Plus, the continuous movement increases the time your muscles are under tension, improving your size and strength gains.

[Legs of Steel](https://longevity.peppysun.com/tag/elamipretide-injection/faq/)

Here’s how to do it: Stand a couple feet in front a bench. Rest the top of one foot behind you on the bench. Keeping your back straight and torso upright, lower your body until your front thigh is parallel to the floor. Slowly push back up to a standing position. That's 1 rep. You should perform the exercise as a continuous movement. If you stop at any point, finish counting your reps. Do as many reps as you can on one leg before repeating with your other leg.
