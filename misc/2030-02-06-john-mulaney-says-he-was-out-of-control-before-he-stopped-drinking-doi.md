# John Mulaney Says He Was ‘Out of Control’ Before He Stopped Drinking, Doing Cocaine

John Mulaney detailed his past with substance abuse in a new Esquire cover story.

Mulaney has often referenced his past experiences with drugs and alcohol in his stand-up comedy.

"This is the physical feat I can do. Three Vicodin and a tequila and I’m still standing," he recalls once thinking."Who’s the athlete now?"

John Mulaney has made a lot of people laugh by telling stories about partying, doing drugs, acquiring drugs, and more. He's sober now, but the stand-up comedian is starting to open up about the thought process that led him down the road to addiction earlier in life.

[of Control Before He](https://7161777.com/7a7h04uk/)

According to Esquire's October cover story, Mulaney began drinking at age 13. "I drank for attention," he said. "I was really outgoing, and then at twelve, I wasn’t. I didn’t know how to act. And then I was drinking, and I was hilarious again."

Mulaney's drinking also prompted him to start using drugs. He says that while he never liked smoking pot, he "loved" cocaine and dabbled with prescription drugs. "I wasn’t a good athlete, so maybe it was some young male thing of This is the physical feat I can do. Three Vicodin and a tequila and I’m still standing," he said. "Who’s the athlete now?"

Mulaney's stand-up material has broached his past substance abuse, but he's rarely gone into detail. In his 2012 special New in Town, he told stories about his wild days, but brushed past the recovery aspect.

"I don’t drink," he says in the special. "I used to drink, then I drank too much, and I had to stop. That surprises a lot of audiences, because I don’t look like someone who used to do anything."

[He Was Out](https://peplawllc.com/)

[He Stopped Drinking](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/blog/9276224478.html)

After years of blacking out, acting obnoxiously, and even drinking perfume (an anecdote he relays in New In Town), the tipping point for Mulaney seems to have been a 2005 bender when he was 23 years old. He told Esquire that particular weekend, he faded in and out like a movie, and he decided he needed to make a dramatic change. "I was like, You’re fucking out of control," he said. "And I thought to myself, I don’t like this guy anymore. I’m not rooting for him."

Mulaney said he stopped doing cocaine and drinking alcohol within a month of one another. He didn't use a program, like Alcoholics Anonymous, either—he shared he was able to quit cold turkey, and he's been sober ever since.

For much of this year, Mulaney has been seen hanging around with fellow comedian and SNL star Pete Davidson. Mulaney has said before that one of his goals in the friendship has been to show Davidson that "you can have a life in comedy that is not insane—a sober, domestic life."

Given Mulaney's own transformation from wild child into the sober, buttoned-up John Mulaney that we see today, his insight definitely comes from a strong place of relation.
