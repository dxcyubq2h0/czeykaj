# 'Game of Thrones' and 'Us' Are Surprisingly Connected, Lupita Nyong'o Shares

The following contains spoilers for 'Game of Thrones' and 'Us.' Read at your own risk!

No disrespect to Steve Rogers and Tony Stark, but the most compelling pop culture moments of 2019 have arrived courtesy of an epic fantasy saga chronicling humanity's self-defeating thirst for power and a horror movie replete with social commentary. We're talking, of course, about Game of Thrones and Us. And while both have spawned countless fan theories and memes, the two juggernauts mostly remained separate until Sunday night.

Lupita Nyong'o, who pulls double duty in Us as both Adelaide Wilson and Red, shared a few comparisons on Twitter between her film's central antagonists, the Tethered, and Game of Thrones' existential threat, the White Walkers.

Nyong'o notes that both the Tethered and White Walkers arrive in eerie silence and share the same basic motivation: annihilate the human race. She also pointed out their similar fashion sensibilities (all-red for the Tethered, all-blue for the wights) and reliance on a signature weapon (golden scissors vs. badass Ice Blade).

Both villains are formidable opponents in a staring contest, and perhaps could benefit from better grooming and nutrition.

But Nyong'o was also quick to highlight the major differences between the Tethered and the White Walkers.

The Tethered, who show up to the Wilsons' lake house unannounced, are far stealthier than the White Walkers, who march across open fields with little regard for the element of surprise. (It helps that whoever they kill can be instantly recruited into the Army of the Dead).

There are also more elemental differences regarding the Tethered's fascination with fire versus the White Walker's ability to wield ice as a weapon.

Oh, and Nyong'0 reminds Us fans that the Tethered are "friends of PETA" who set their rabbit friends free before emerging from underground to wreak havoc. On the other hand, White Walkers are pretty dickish to animals, as evidenced by The Night King flexing on his favorite Westerosi Olympic sport, Ice Javelin, to zombify Daenerys' dragons.

Nyong'o didn't speculate about which makes for the better villain, but because The Night King pretty much sucks, we have to give it to Red and her merry band of psychopathic clone murderers.
