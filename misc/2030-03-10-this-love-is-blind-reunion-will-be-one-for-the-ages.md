# This ‘Love Is Blind’ Reunion Will Be One For the Ages

EACH SEASON OF Love Is Blind is a rollercoaster, both for its contestants and the viewers. You root for relationships as they form in the pods, and then watch as, for various reasons, they fall apart when faced with the pains of the real world. If Netflix really is trying to run an experiment on whether attractiveness matters in a relationship, it's safe to say it's definitely failed. But beyond the original premise of the show, it's at least interesting to see how regular people fare when dating apps and set-ups from mutual friends no longer exist. It's speed dating on another level.

Season 6 had both happy endings and shocking breakups. When the couples exited the pods, there were 5 total set to be married at the end of the show. But as life continued outside of the pods, and partners introduced one another to their parents and friends, and even exes from the pods, things got very, very complicated.
