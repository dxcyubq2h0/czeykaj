Haley Lacey, MSN, APRN, AGPCNP-C, WHNP-BC

Called to care: "I've been caring for women throughout my entire career. I became a nurse in 2011, beginning in labor and delivery before gaining experience in the operating room and as a lactation consultant. After becoming a nurse practitioner in 2016, I worked in gynecologic oncology and maternal-fetal medicine. Since 2022, I've focused primarily on hormone replacement therapy and weight management. Joining Midi felt like a natural fit because caring for women, especially during midlife, is truly my calling."

Changing the conversation: "I'm passionate about helping rewrite the story around hormone replacement therapy. For too long, many women have gone without effective treatment because of outdated misconceptions. I love helping patients understand their options, make informed decisions, and discover just how much better they can feel."

More than a visit: "Someone once told me that if you find something you'd do for free, you'll never work a day in your life. That's exactly how I feel about this work. The most rewarding part is seeing patients come back feeling like themselves again and telling me their lives have changed."

Every woman matters: "I want every patient to know they're never just another number. I take the time to listen, understand their goals, and create a plan that fits their life. My hope is that every woman leaves our visit feeling heard, supported, and confident that I truly care about helping her live a healthier, more fulfilled life."
