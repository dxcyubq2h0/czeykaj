What is Functional Dyspepsia?

A condition where symptoms like pain, bloating or indigestion persist without any obvious cause identified.

Typical Symptoms of Functional Dyspepsia

Abdominal bloating after meals

Diagnostic Questions for Functional Dyspepsia

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have abdominal bloating after meals?

Do you have heartburn with discomfort in your upper stomach area?

Do you feel pain in your upper abdomen?

Do you have frequent stomach or abdominal pain with unknown causes?

Do you have heartburn with a burning sensation in your upper abdomen or hunger-like pain?

Treatment of Functional Dyspepsia

The doctor may prescribe gas remedies, medications to reduce or block acid production, antibiotics, medications to help your stomach empty faster, anti-nausea pills, or low-dose antidepressants. Diet and lifestyle changes or behavioral therapy may also be recommended.
