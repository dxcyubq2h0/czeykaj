# Jamie Dornan Stripped Down to His Underwear for a Polar Bear Plunge

For many, the holidays are a time to take a well-earned break from work, enjoy some of our favorite festive foods, and maybe ease up temporarily on our workout routines. Jamie Dornan has another approach: taking a dip in freezing cold ocean water. Which is certainly one way to wake yourself up from a food coma.

[for Polar Bear Plunge](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/)

The actor shared a short clip on his Instagram page of himself, stripped to just his underwear on the beach in Ireland, wishing his followers a "merry Christmas" before charging into the sea. It's the exact same thing that movie star Hugh Jackman did this time last year to see in the New Year; it's called a polar bear plunge, and while on the surface it sounds bracing at best and miserable at worst, there's a pretty good reason to do it.

[Underwear for Polar](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/blog/7453774469.html)

Exposing the body to sudden, drastic changes in temperature has been found to offer a variety of benefits. Pro surfer Laird Hamilton uses extreme hot and cold exposure as part of his morning routine to shock his system. "It's going to rev you up," he says.

Some people take it even further; Wim Hof, aka the "Ice Man" and proponent of the Wim Hof Method, has explored prolonged exposure to cold temperatures as a way of improving mood and managing anxiety, as well as a range of physical uses such as aiding in quicker muscle recovery following intense training.
