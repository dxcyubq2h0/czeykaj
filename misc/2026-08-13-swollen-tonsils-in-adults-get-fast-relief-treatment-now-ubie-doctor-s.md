Explanation

Swollen tonsils, or tonsillitis, in adults can be uncomfortable and may require treatment. One option for treating recurrent acute tonsillitis is the antibiotic clindamycin. This medication is used to fight the bacteria causing the infection, helping to reduce swelling and symptoms.

Another common approach to treating acute tonsillitis involves other antibiotics, as these medications help clear the infection. However, the choice of antibiotic may depend on the specific bacteria causing the tonsillitis. For some adults with recurrent tonsillitis, doctors may consider different antibiotics based on the patient's history and the bacteria involved.

It's important to follow a healthcare provider's advice on which treatment is best. They will consider factors like the frequency of tonsillitis episodes and the specific needs of the patient.
