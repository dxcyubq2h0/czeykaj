Explanation

Pain in the left ankle can happen for different reasons. Common causes include ankle sprains, which occur when the ligaments are stretched or torn. This is often due to twisting the ankle. Other common reasons are arthritis, which can cause joint pain and swelling, and tendonitis, which is inflammation of the tendons around the ankle.

Uncommon causes can include nerve damage, which might cause sharp or burning pain, and injuries to the cartilage or bones within the ankle joint. In older adults, like in the case study of a 75-year-old, conditions such as arthritis are more prevalent, but unusual causes should not be overlooked.

When evaluating ankle pain, it is important to consider both common and less common causes to ensure proper treatment.
