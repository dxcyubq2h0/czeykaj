# Chrissy from 'Stranger Things 4' Is a Huge Gaming Streamer

Stranger Things 4 is the scariest yet. Sure, we’ve watched Will, Mike and the rest of the gang put their lives at risk to stop Russian scientists and hellish monsters who have a strange desire to kill every kid in Hawkins. But the Vecna is the creepiest of them all. He's got a humanoid appearance, a terrifying origin story, and is determined to use children’s trauma against them in a Freddy Kreuger-ish way.

Vecna lures in his victims by learning a person’s secret, haunting them with it, then dragging them into the Upside Down, where he kills them. In the regular world, it looks like the person’s been possessed. Floating in the air, eyes rolled in the back of the head. And when the Vecna’s definitely got them, their bones begin to crack and their body contorts into unnatural ways. For a family-friendly show, that’s some heavy stuff.

Throughout Season 4 Part 1 (Part 2 is set to release on July 1), Vecna manages to get a few Hawkins High students, starting with poor Chrissy Cunningham. We first meet the cheerleader in Episode 1, where she’s seen as the All-American girlfriend of the basketball team captain, Jason.

Although we didn’t get much time with Chrissy, we saw Vecna dig up her fraught relationship with her mother and we also glimpsed a budding friendship (or maybe even relationship) with Eddie (Joseph Quinn), the gang’s Dungeons & Dragons dungeon master. She didn’t last long; by the end of the first episode, Chrissy is dead, and Hawkins falls into disarray at her demise.

Who Plays Chrissy Cunningham in Stranger Things Season 4?

Chrissy Cunningham is played by Grace Van Dien. The 26-year-old actress had her first role in 2010 for a TV Christmas movie. Since then, she’s starred in various roles in movies and on television shows, including the Charles Manson-based film Charlie Says, where she played Sharon Tate. She’s also starred in another Netflix teen series, Greenhouse Academy.

According to Marie Claire, Van Dien got into acting because of her parents. Her mother is Carrie Mitchum of The Bold and the Beautiful, and her father is Casper Van Dien who starred in All American and Starship Troopers.

[Things Is Huge](https://7161777.com/u7kxzr/)

Recently, the actress also trended online for her Twitch streams, where she plays battle royale games like Overwatch and Valorant.

According to IGN India, Van Dien has exploded on Twitch since the show. Before Stranger Things, Van Dien averaged less than 100 views on her videos. Now? The views are in the thousands. Looks like she has a second career path if she ever decides to leave acting.

On filming Chrissy's gruesome death scene, Joseph Quinn (who plays Eddie on Stranger Things) told Men's Health it was far less scary in person.

"I’m seeing Grace very unelegantly suspended in the air in a harness, complaining," Quinn says. "But then there was an amazing stunt where Chrissy’s character gets slapped up onto the ceiling. But there were no bones broken, or anything like that. It was very methodical, and it took a while. But the end result is terrifying, isn’t it?"

There's no doubt about that.
