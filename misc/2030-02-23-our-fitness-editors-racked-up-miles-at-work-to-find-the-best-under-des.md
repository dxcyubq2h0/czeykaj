Under desk bikes are practical, stealthy tools that quietly combat the risks of sitting too long, much like under desk treadmills. Got a standing desk? Even better. An under desk bike works best when paired with a quality standing desk because the adjustable height gives you the clearance to pedal comfortably without hitting your knees. These compact pedaling machines target your quads, hamstrings, and glutes, giving your lower body a solid workout while you tackle emails or power through that afternoon videoconference marathon. But not all under desk bikes are created equal. Some are like late-night infomercial gadgets—exciting at first but quickly forgotten. Here’s what to look for when picking the perfect one for your workspace.

What to Consider

Stability

Under desk bikes are lighter by default, so stability is a valid concern. Choose a model that's secure and heavy enough to stay put during use, and consider using a non-slip mat underneath for the best experience. "Look to see if the machine comes with a non-slip pad, an anchoring strap, or choose one that weighs more so it stays put," says Dr. Malpelli.

Noise

Considering using the machine during work? Opt for a model that isn't too noisy, especially if you'll be taking phone or video calls. A quiet under desk bike ensures you can seamlessly integrate your workout into your work routine without distractions.

Adjustability

Resistance settings and adjustable pedal height are essential for finding the perfect fit. The ideal bike should integrate smoothly with your desk setup while offering the right level of challenge.

Ease of Use

Keep it simple. You need something intuitive that fits right into your office routine. Nobody wants to spend hours setting up a fitness machine either, so check the setup process before purchasing. Some under desk bikes come fully assembled, while some require a little more work to get up and running.

Resistance

Look for models with adjustable resistance to scale up the intensity of your workouts. This feature is crucial for building strength while pedaling or striding. Ensure your under-desk exerciser allows you to customize your workouts to make them more challenging.

Storage and Portability

Small spaces demand smart solutions. A compact bike that’s easy to move or store is a must for keeping your workspace uncluttered and functional.

Benefits of an Under Desk Bike

An under desk bike is not a replacement for a workout, and because you're not never truly decelerating your body, as you do when taking a walk, it won't have profound impacts on overall movement markers like balance or locomotion, says fitness director Ebenezer Samuel, C.S.C.S. But you will drive lower-body bloodflow and circulation. There's no downside to an under-desk bike, but you're not using this for calorie burn, either.

Research from sources like the American Council on Exercise suggests that incorporating physical activity, even in the form of under desk cycling, can even enhance cognitive function and focus. By pedaling discreetly under your desk, you're not just getting a workout—you're potentially boosting your mental clarity and overall well-being. It's a win-win for your body and your mind.

How Does an Under Desk Bike Compare to a Treadmill?

When comparing under desk bikes to under desk treadmills, the key distinction is in your seating arrangement (or lack thereof). Under desk bikes allow you to comfortably sit, offering a lower-impact option.

Under desk treadmills, on the other hand, may burn more calories as they require you to move your whole body, but they come with a trade-off: standing. They are also larger and heavier, making them less maneuverable.

Plus, we've found that the potential noise from walking on a treadmill is typically greater than that of an under desk bike. So, if you prefer a seated, lower-impact experience that's gentler, quieter, and more compact, an under desk bike might be your pedal-perfect choice.
