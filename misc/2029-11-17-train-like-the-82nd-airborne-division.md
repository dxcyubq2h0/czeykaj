In the U.S. Army, soldier development training isn’t what it used to be—today it covers mind, body, and spirit. Officially launched in 2020, the Army’s Holistic Health and Fitness (H2F) system is a whole-person approach to preparing soldiers for their most important missions and their everyday lives.

No place is H2F better displayed than within the 82nd Airborne Division—an elite division of the U.S. Army responsible for deploying to military operations via parachute, within 18 hours of notification. To successfully carry out their missions, 82nd Airborne paratroopers need to be ready, mentally and physically, at all times— which is why H2F is such a critical part of their training.

H2F consists of five main pillars that collectively enhance the paratroopers’ emotional and physical resilience, stress tolerance, and leadership skills. The men and women of the 82nd Airborne train within these areas on a daily basis. Specifically, those pillars are:

Physical—this area covers all elements of strength, conditioning, mobility, and endurance.

Mental—this training teaches soldiers to calm their minds and enhance focus with activities like meditation and yoga.
