# This Guy Got Surgery For His Thinning Hair. Here’s What He Looks Like a Year Later.

Thinning hair and male pattern baldness can have a huge impact on a guy's confidence and self-image. There are a number of different ways you can approach the issue, from grooming and styling tricks that minimize the visibility of bald patches, to more extreme measures, such as drugs and surgical procedures.

In a recent video, Zach from YouTube's Try Guys shared an update of his progress one year after getting hair restoration surgery at Barber Surgeons Guild in Los Angeles.

[Surgery For His Thinning](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/blog/8975869789.html)

"I don't think there's anything wrong with losing your hair or being bald," he says. "A lot of guys look great with it. Every action star ever. Dwayne Johnson, Vin Diesel, Bruce Willis, Jason Statham... But unfortunately for me, I didn't feel good about it. For a long time my hair has been just a pure source of insecurity."

Zach's issue was hair loss as well as active thinning in his hair follicles, and so in addition to the surgery and scalp camouflage (where portions of the scalp are tattooed to create the illusion of having thicker hair), he started taking finasteride, which actually stimulated his hair to start repairing itself in places, meaning he only required surgical intervention in a couple of places.

"A lot of my hair was salvageable, and so getting on medicine has helped thicken it and boost my follicles again," he says. "I was terrified of that surgery, for a lot of reasons. I didn't want a big scar on the back of my head. It seemed painful and like the recovery would suck... The procedure we did left me with no scar whatsoever. It was a totally easy, pain-free procedure."
