# In Case You Were Wondering, Here’s Everyone Who Dies in 'The Suicide Squad'

The following story, obviously, is pure spoilers for The Suicide Squad. If you haven't watched the movie—or somehow haven't come to this story with the express purpose of spoiling yourself—then please stop reading here!

[You Were](https://pepvoice.com/)

I mean, look: it's already in the name, right? The damn movie is called The Suicide Squad. It's the sequel/reboot to Suicide Squad. The first version of the Suicide Squad debuted in the DC Comics all the way back in 1959. People in this movie were always going to die. And when the movie landed a Hard-R rating, and James Gunn—returning to his bloody, pulpy, Super and Slither roots—as director, you had to expect some outlandish death scenes.

And yet some of these still came as a massive surprise. That's right—in a movie literally called The Suicide Squad, some of these character deaths really ended up catching us off guard. Was it because of strong performances? In some cases, yes. Was it because of pure, utter shock? In some cases, yes. Was it sometimes just completely fucking bonkers in a way that maybe only James Gunn could pull it of? In some cases, maybe, yes.

Also, again, let's just be real for a second. This is The Suicide Squad. I'm only charting the characters who died here who, you know, had a name and some sort of character identity. I'm not about to start detailing the death of the guy who died because Bloodsport (Idris Elba) shot an electric fan and it fell on his penis (which, yes, really happens). Nor am I about to chart the death of the guy who gets his head sliced open by a boomerang in the opening moments of the movie (again, yes, actually happens). Let's stay focused here, people.

Either way, that's enough beating around the bush for now. You want to know who died in The Suicide Squad, so we're going to let you know who died in The Suicide Squad.

Javelin (Flula Borg)

How did he die? Ironically, just shot while he tried throwing his javelin. Not the most efficient of skills, as it turns out! At least he passed the Javelin off to Harley Quinn for.....some reason.

T.D.K. (Nathan Fillion)

How did he die? Did he die? It seemed to say he was in critical condition. But his detachable arms (TDK stands for...The Detachable Kid) were getting absolutely destroyed, while his body was writhing in pain. Didn't look great!

Savant (Michael Rooker)

How did he die? The first character we see in the movie (and the last one to die in this blowout opening sequence) got his head blown up remotely by Amanda Waller (Viola Davis) after trying to desert the mission. Michael Rooker with long, flowing hair—we hardly knew you.

Silvio Luna (Juan Diego Botto)

How did he die? This wannabe dictator villain clown was on some half-assed evil villain rant, and while he was wooing Harley Quinn, it wasn't long after he was talking about killing children that she shot him right in the heart. Moron.

The Thinker (Peter Capaldi)

How did he die? I won't say we hardly knew you to this guy, because quite frankly he was pretty annoying. His pet, of sorts, turned out to be Starro the Conqueror (who we'll talk about in a little bit). Anyway, Starro had his way with The Thinker, ripping him apart limb by limb before the rest of his body got completely liquified being thrown into a window.

Col. Rick Flag (Joel Kinnaman)

How did he die? This one hurt. When Flag and everyone else discovered that the United States was actually behind Project Starfish, and their mission from Waller was actually to destroy the evidence, Flag planned to take it to the press and go public with the information. At this point, it became apparant that Peacemaker was Waller's failsafe, and he would not be allowing Flag to do this. The two had an incredible fight scene, that unfortunately ended with Peacemaker stabbing Flag directly into the heart. His final line might just be the movie's most memorable moment: "Peacemaker...what a joke."

Peacemaker (John Cena)

How did he die? Almost a Western-style duel with Bloodsport. Bloodsport's smaller bullet went directly through Peacemaker's, and shot him in the neck. He's dead! Or....is he.... (You've got to stay tuned for that post-credits scene, obviously.)

Milton (Julio Ruiz)

How did he die? POOR MILTON. Milton was just a guy trying to help. And Harley Quinn didn't even know who he was! Poor Polka-Dot Man. He loved him! Milton got shot. WE LOST MILTON!
