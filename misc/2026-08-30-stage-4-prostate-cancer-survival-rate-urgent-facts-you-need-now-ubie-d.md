Stage 4 prostate cancer survival rates vary widely depending on where the cancer has spread, the patient's age, overall health, and response to treatment. On average, the 5-year relative survival rate for metastatic (stage 4) prostate cancer is approximately 30-35%, which is significantly lower than earlier stages, where survival rates often exceed 95%. Factors influencing prognosis include whether the cancer has spread to nearby lymph nodes, bones, or distant organs, as well as the effectiveness of therapies such as hormone therapy, chemotherapy, and newer targeted treatments.
