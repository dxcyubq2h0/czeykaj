Enhanced Clinics Pricing

Enhanced Clinics often advertises “all-in pricing”: the listed price is for the therapy plan, including the consultation, prescription, and shipping. Their model does not rely on insurance. Users pay directly. There is no insurance billing involved. They claim their pricing is often lower than that of typical pharmacies or similar services.

The pricing is in US dollars (USD) and applies only to customers in the United States. The website’s shipping/fulfillment is restricted to U.S. addresses. For specific treatments, ongoing monthly payments are required beyond the first month.

They do not accept health insurance. Payment is made directly out of pocket by the user via the credit card methods listed. Their pricing is presented up-front (no hidden service fees or surprise billing). The cost for consultation, prescription, and shipping is included in the displayed price.

Enhanced Clinics Shipping

For a user in the U.S., Enhanced Clinics offers a relatively streamlined and convenient tele-health with home-delivery model, especially useful if they want prescription or wellness products without visiting a physical clinic or pharmacy. The inclusion of 2-day priority shipping and no-contact delivery reduces typical hassles associated with prescriptions.

However, for people outside the U.S. or seeking medication internationally, this model isn’t accessible, a limitation worth highlighting. Also, because returns are not allowed for prescription medication, users should be sure of their order before purchasing.

Enhanced Clinics’ Money Back Policy

The “100% Money‑Back Guarantee” that Enhance.MD advertises is not a general “satisfaction guarantee” or “if you don’t like it, you can return it.” It specifically applies only to cases where the provider deems you ineligible before medication is shipped.

Once medication is shipped, you should assume no refund / no return is possible. Same as with a regular pharmacy purchase, under U.S. prescription‑med law.

For prospective patients: Before ordering, ensure you are comfortable with their evaluation and medication use— once shipped, there’s no going back.
