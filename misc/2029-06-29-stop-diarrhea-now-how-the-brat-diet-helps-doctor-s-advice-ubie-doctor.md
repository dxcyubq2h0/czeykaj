How does the BRAT diet help with diarrhea management?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

The BRAT diet—bananas, rice, applesauce, and toast—helps manage diarrhea by offering bland, low-fiber foods that are easy to digest and can help firm up loose stools. These starchy, gentle foods reduce stomach irritation, replace lost nutrients like potassium, and give the digestive system time to recover during bouts of gastrointestinal upset.

However, diarrhea can stem from many causes—viral infections, food intolerances, medications, or underlying conditions—and the right next step depends on what's actually driving your symptoms.
