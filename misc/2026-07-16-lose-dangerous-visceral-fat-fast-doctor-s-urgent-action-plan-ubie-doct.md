How can I effectively reduce visceral fat?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Visceral fat—the deep belly fat surrounding your organs—can be reduced through a combination of regular exercise and targeted dietary changes. Effective strategies include aerobic activity (like brisk walking, running, or cycling), strength training, and following a diet rich in lean protein, fiber, and healthy fats while limiting refined sugars and processed carbs. Consistency across both movement and nutrition is key to seeing measurable results.
