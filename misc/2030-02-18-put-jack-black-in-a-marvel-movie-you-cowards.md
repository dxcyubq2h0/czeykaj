# Put Jack Black in a Marvel Movie, You Cowards

[Movie You](https://peplawllc.com/blog/3071750232.html)

Jack Black expressed interest in someday playing Marvel characters The Kingpin or The Thing in an interview with ScreenRant.

[Black in Marvel](https://antimicrobial-hub.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/3048004225.html)

[Black in Marvel](https://bioresearch.wiki/oue26c/)

Um... WHY NOT?

Jack Black is film royalty, and should be treated as such. Put him in a Marvel movie. Come on. Do it.

[in Marvel Movie](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Let's not beat around the bush: Jack Black is a pure ray of shining light in the world. With so much to not feel great about, Jack Black exists and succeeds at exuding positive energy, whether it's in your 200th rewatch of The School of Rock, coming across Tropic Thunder on cable, or clicking the link to that Tenacious D music video your friend from high school sent last week. So when word got out that Mr. Black was even talking about the hypothetical prospect of being in a Marvel Cinematic Universe movie, well, that's some good energy right there.

The 50-year-old actor and, now, YouTuber, was discussing his role in the new Jumanji movie in a conversation with ScreenRant when the topic of the Marvel Cinematic Universe came up. "It’d be fun to play like an evil dude, like the Kingpin," Black said. "Yeah. I don’t know. Maybe The Thing? It’d be rad to be that rock dude from the Fantastic Four."

So, okay. We can be real for a second. This is 100% just a fun, playful quote. We repeat: Jack Black, as far as we know, is not in any discussions to appear in a Marvel movie. But, as a simple follow-up to that sentiment, uh, why not? Jack Black isn't just a wonderful source of joy to all, but he's also a great actor. Even aside from School of Rock, which made eternally-perfect use of Black's aggressive energy, he's appeared in a number of roles that not only were enjoyable to watch, but that showcased his skill as a performer as well.

[You Cowards](https://yldzdh.com/arfp740zdz/)

One, for instance, is his role as the director Carl Denham in the not-often-discussed anymore 2005 remake of King Kong. Director Peter Jackson (Lord of the Rings trilogy) trusted Black to essentially anchor his first post-LOTR project as the greedy, selfish, gross director leading the expedition to see Kong, and Black is up for the challenge, as always. While not quite a super-villain here, his scummy, untrustworthy energy is something that a future movie and a creative director could certainly tap into.

There's also Bernie, which saw Black re-team with Richard Linklater (who helmed School of Rock). Bernie is a based-on-a-true-story movie about an odd man (Black) who is so embedded in small town culture, and seemed like such a nice guy, that the entire community refused to believe that he would...murder a little old lady, whom he was employed by (which, you see, he very much did). It's a stunning performance from Black, and a total left turn—the type that could certainly let you see a villainous transformation in a future movie if you squint hard enough.

And that's why Black's (certainly not entirely serious) comments should, in fact, be taken seriously: the guy isn't just a joke (though he's very funny), and he's willing to try anything. After all, in the first Jumanji movie, he literally played a teenage girl—our guy isn't going to back down from a challenge.

We've seen a number of takes on the Kingpin, from Michael Clarke Duncan in 2003's Ben Affleck Daredevil (not good), to Vincent D'Onofrio's in Netflix's Daredevil and Liev Schrieber's in Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (both good), so maybe it would make more sense to give Black a shot to make a lesser-known character his own. Maybe let him try something out first in a Disney+ series, and if it pays off, then take the next step to a cinematic film. Whatever.

[Marvel Movie You Cowards](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/blog/5691503293.html)

With 23 Marvel movies in the horn, and almost certainly a few more coming down the pipeline, why not take a shot on Jack Black as a villain in one? Seriously. Why the hell not? The DC world hasn't shied away from this type of risk: the main villain in next year's Wonder Woman 1984, Cheetah, will be played by....Kristen Wiig. So, again, Marvel—why not? What are you afraid of? Cast Jack Black as a villain! You know you want to do it. Come on.
