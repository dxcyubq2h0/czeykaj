Sciatica treatment typically includes non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) such as ibuprofen or naproxen to reduce inflammation and relieve pain. Doctors may also prescribe muscle relaxants, oral corticosteroids, nerve pain medications like gabapentin, or, in more severe cases, opioids for short-term relief. Treatment plans vary based on the severity of symptoms, underlying causes, and individual health factors.

Sciatica is a condition that causes pain along the sciatic nerve, which runs down the back of the leg. To help with this pain, doctors often prescribe medications. One common type of medication is non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs). These drugs help reduce inflammation and pain.

According to the studies, NSAIDs can be helpful for some people with sciatica, but they might not work for everyone. Other pain relief medications are also used, and the choice of drug can depend on how severe the pain is and how the person responds to treatment.
