🧪 What is Biohacking?

[The Morning Dose GHK-Cu](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/blog/3063606302.html)

Photo by John Arano on Unsplash

Biohacking is a growing movement rooted in the idea of using science, experimentation, and self-monitoring to optimize the body and mind. It involves making strategic lifestyle, dietary, and technological changes to improve overall performance, well-being, and longevity. Whether it’s through nootropics, wearable tech, or controlled routines like intermittent fasting, biohackers aim to take control of their own biology.

Some of the most common methods include tracking sleep quality, experimenting with supplements, adjusting macronutrient ratios, and even using light therapy or cold exposure to test physical limits. More advanced forms may involve genetic testing or implantable tech to gather precise data on bodily functions. The key is personalization—biohacking isn’t a one-size-fits-all approach but a continuous process of learning and refining based on results.
