# 10 Gift Ideas that Stand Out This Holiday Season

Gift-giving isn’t a competition — it’s a connection. The right gift says you noticed, you remembered, and you actually thought about what might make someone smile. That’s what matters.

If you’re ready to unlock the next level of things, though, you are going to have to work a little harder. To level up your gift game, focus on its presents that are unique or show you’re really thinking about the recipient’s hobbies or passions. These aren’t gifts you can order in bulk, quickly wrapping up the entire shopping season in one go. Rather, they’re special, just-for-them finds that will stand out.

Adventurous buddy who would rather be outside, always? There's something for him in here. The most stylish guy in the group who you know cares more about his accessories than he’d ever let on? We’ve got you covered thanks to finds from Fossil’s latest styles.

From gifts for the pet to a sleeping pick that might just change the way he wakes up in the morning, these 10 finds are a very good spot to kick off your holiday shopping.

Fossil Carraway Automatic Brown Croco Leather Watch

Even non-watch guys know that there’s a lot of intricate engineering built into something so small, but typical designs don’t let you peek at what’s really going on. This skeletonized style fixes that problem with a rectangular cutout window front and center. Wrap it up for the natty dresser who is also just a little bit nerdy about stuff like this.

YETI Shot Glasses

Beef up a buddy’s collection of tumblers and help them always be prepared for fun times with this insulated set. A carrying case is included so they can easily toss these in a catch-all bag, which means they are perfect for camping, kayaking outings, and tailgates.

Therabody Therabody SleepMask

This isn’t your average sleep mask. Designed to block out 100 percent of light, it’s serious when it comes to casting someone into total darkness. Beyond that, the lucky giftee can pick from three different (and adjustable) vibration sensations designed to induce deep sleep.

Orilla Sun Hat

Baseball caps are great, but a wider, 360 brim is what’s really required for full neck and face coverage during outdoor adventures. Made of 100 percent recycled polyester, this rollable, travel-friendly style is based on the original classic shape but has design details that let it stand out from the crowd. Wrap it up for the fishers, hikers, and yard-obsessives in your life.

Built for the guy who likes gear that works as hard as he does. Inspired by classic American machinery, the Machine Chronograph makes a statement with its bold 44mm case, knurled topring, and brushed black dial. The stainless steel bracelet delivers just the right amount of shine, while a quartz chronograph movement keeps things precise. Tough, refined, and unapologetically masculine—engrave the caseback with something that lasts as long as the watch itself.

Red Wing Boot Jack

One of the best gifts is something they don’t even know they needed. Come boot season, that means a jack designed to make taking a pair off easy (as in, no bending down and tugging). It’s practical, yes, but don’t confuse that with boring. They’ll appreciate this present every single time they use it.
