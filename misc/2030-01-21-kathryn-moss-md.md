Kathryn Moss, MD

Dr. Moss has been in practice for thirty years. She completed her training at New York Presbyterian Weill Cornell and did a fellowship in Emergency Psychiatry at New York University College of Medicine, Bellevue Hospital Center. She is Board Certified in

Psychiatry.

Dr. Moss has worked in both private and community-based outpatient settings specializing in women’s mental health. She has developed a specialty in menopause, and the psychiatric symptoms that can occur during all phases of menopause.
