# 8 Facts You Might Not Know About "The White Lotus"

Season 2 of The White Lotus made quite a splash, arguably even more so than the first season. With an entirely new cast and setting, we had to wonder what exactly this show was up to– and it did not disappoint.

But if you binged watch the latest season, you may have missed some of the clever references, cameos, and fashion statements that are baked into the show. Not to worry, because we did the homework for you. Here are eight facts you might not know about The White Lotus.

Jennifer Coolidge’s character was meant to tie the two seasons together

What do the two seasons of The White Lotus have in common? Jennifer Coolidge. According to the writer and director, Mike White, Coolidge’s character Tanya was brought into season 2 so viewers could feel some sort of connection between seasons. Her husband Greg, who she met in Hawaii, is also along for the ride here. While all of The White Lotus season 1 characters were great, we’re glad Tanya’s over-the-top personality got to stay on.

Harper is the only character Aubrey Plaza has played that she really relates to

Plaza is well known for her role in Parks & Recreation, where she plays a somewhat dry and uninterested intern. Lawyer Harper in The White Lotus is quite different from Parks' April Ludgate. Plaza told GQ that she really identifies with Harper, noting that it’s the only role that felt very similar to her real life.

Fashion stole the show this season, with iconic (and controversial) looks from Portia to Tanya’s pink, flowy dresses. But you might not have guessed that some of these looks were found from local vintage shops in Italy. In an interview with People, designer Alex Bovaird revealed that Lucia’s stunning red cutout dress was spontaneously purchased in a vintage shop in Catania, Italy.

Both seasons were filmed at Four Seasons hotels

The Four Seasons is 2/2 with The White Lotus, so we can only expect another Four Seasons to take the stage in Season 3. While the first season was filmed at a Four Seasons in Hawaii, the second was filmed at San Domenico Palace, a Four Seasons-partnered hotel in Taormina, Sicily.

Contestants from the reality show Survivor make a cameo

If you’re not familiar with the competition series Survivor, you may have missed Angelina Keeley and Kara Kay’s cameos in the first episode. But why are reality TV show contestants on a dark drama like The White Lotus? Well, besides the fact that the show’s writer and director Mike White competed on the Survivor, we think the reference hints at Daphne’s strength, which becomes apparent as the season goes on.

Love Island was another fashion inspiration for the show

Reality show Love Island was largely used to inform Jack, Leo Woodall’s character. Costume designer Alex Bovaird even admits to entirely basing Jack’s style on the popular TV show, according to Vogue. An avid watcher of Love Island herself, Bovaird was able to turn Jack’s obnoxious personality into a familiar, dating show-esque style we could appreciate.

HBO is responsible for the show existing at all

Not only does HBO air The White Lotus, but the network actually kick started the creation of the show. White told The New York Times that HBO reached out to him, asking for an idea for a show that could be filmed during COVID restrictions. In classic Mike White genius fashion, the inventive director came up with what we have today.

Season 3 might take place in Asia

We’ve followed the show from Hawaii to Europe, and rumor has it Season 3 will take place in Asia. In an after-show scene of the Season 2 finale, White said he’d like to explore Eastern religion and spirituality in the next season. We’ll be patiently awaiting the next masterpiece!
