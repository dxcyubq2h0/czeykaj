# '28 Years Later: The Bone Temple' Won't Be the End. Danny Boyle and Alex Garland's Story Will Go On.

The following story contains spoilers for 28 Years Later and 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple.

IT'S A GOOD time to be a fan of horror movies, because less than a year after one of the best director/writer collaborations of the last 25 years gave us a stone cold banger, director Nia DaCosta came in and gave us another one. More than two decades after director Danny Boyle and writer Alex Garland joined forces to give us the low-fi survival horror epic 28 Days Later, they returned for 28 Years Later, and not only did it rock, but it perfectly set up a direct sequel, 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple, which also rocked, and now has us levitating and wondering what comes next. (28 Weeks Later came out in 2007 and is pretty solid, but neither Boyle nor Garland were involved on a writing or directing level.)

[Story Will Go On](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/)

The films follows a young boy named Spike (Alfie Williams) who was born into a world where his home in the U.K. has always been a quarantined post-apocalyptic hellscape. We see him spend time with both his flawed father, Jamie (Aaron Taylor-Johnson) and his loving-but-ailing mother, Isla (Jodie Comer), making for two halves of a movie that comes together in a surprising but fantastic whole.

The second film, The Bone Temple, continues Spike's story while focusing in more closely on a pair of characters we met in the back half of the first 28 Years Later film: the kind, sweet, and thoughtful Dr. Ian Kelson (Ralph Fiennes) and the totally psychopathic Sir Lord Jimmy Crystal (Jack O'Connell).

Both 28 Years Later and 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple end in similar fashion: with a cliffhanger. We talked about this with Danny Boyle last year, and he explained what makes a whole lot of sense—this new 28 Years Later project isn't just one single new movie, and it isn't even two new movies. The plan here is for a whole new trilogy, and the third film, thankfully, is on the way.

Will there be a sequel to 28 Years Later and 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple?

As you may have guessed from the ending, yes, there will be a sequel to 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple, and it will both continue Spike's story and also circle back to the events of the original 28 Days Later film, as Cillian Murphy's Jim is revealed in the final moments of the movie.

While Danny Boyle wasn't sure initially if they were going to get to make the third 28 Years Later movie (it depended on the box office), the movie was officially greenlit in December 2025.

Garland will once again write the script for the third film, and Boyle has suggested that he would like to return to direct it once again himself.

"There's a fully fleshed scenario outlined," Boyle told MH in an interview last year. "And obviously it's a big part for Cillian [Murphy], whose role in the second film is a bit like Jack O'Connell's in the first film. He appears in this captivating ending, beautifully introduced by Nia. It's beautifully set up, and again, you just go, "Oh, God. I really want to see.""

He's right. We do really want to see!

Boyle is currently working on his movie Ink, and would have to finish that up first before moving on to the third 28 Years Later movie. But he suggested last year that the film could theoretically shoot in summer of 2026, which would put it on schedule to release in mid or late 2027.

"These are summer films. They have to be shot in the summer, because they're mostly outdoors, there's no electricity, all that kind of stuff. There are some night scenes, there's a great night sequence in the second film, right at the end, but most of the both films are daytime," he explained in the same MH interview. "And to do that in Britain, you have to shoot in the summer. In theory, it would shoot next summer."
