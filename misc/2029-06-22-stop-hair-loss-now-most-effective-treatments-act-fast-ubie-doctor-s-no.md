What are the most effective treatments for hair loss?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Hair loss treatments vary in effectiveness, but the most proven options include:

Finasteride: An oral prescription medication that blocks DHT, the hormone responsible for male pattern baldness.

Minoxidil: A topical treatment (available over-the-counter) that stimulates hair follicles and promotes regrowth.

Medications

Finasteride: This medication is used for male pattern hair loss. It works by blocking a hormone that causes hair loss. Studies have shown it can be effective in slowing hair loss and even promoting regrowth.

Minoxidil: This is a topical or oral treatment that can be used by both men and women. It helps to increase blood flow to hair follicles, which can help with hair growth.

New Therapies

New research is exploring other treatments for male androgenetic alopecia, which is a common type of hair loss in men. These new therapies are still being studied for their effectiveness and safety.

It's important to talk to a healthcare provider to understand the best treatment options for your specific situation. Each treatment has its own benefits and potential side effects, so professional guidance is crucial.
