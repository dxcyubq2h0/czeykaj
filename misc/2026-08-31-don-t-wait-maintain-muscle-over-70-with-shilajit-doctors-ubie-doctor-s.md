Explanation

As we age, losing muscle mass—known as sarcopenia—becomes more common. After age 70, up to 30–50% of muscle mass can be lost without proper care. Combining evidence-based strategies with safe supplements like shilajit can help you preserve strength, mobility, and quality of life. This guide draws on peer-reviewed studies, clinical expertise, and practical tips to help seniors combat muscle mass loss.

Building a Comprehensive Plan

1. Work Closely with Your Doctor

Review medications and medical conditions before starting any supplement.

Monitor hormone levels, kidney function, and overall health.

Adjust doses or discontinue if side effects occur.

2. Prioritize Resistance Exercise

Frequency: Aim for 2–3 sessions per week.

Squats or chair stands

Seated or standing calf raises

Chest presses with light dumbbells or resistance bands

Rows or pull-downs with bands

Core work: seated twists, pelvic tilts

Intensity: Start at a weight or band resistance you can lift 8–12 times, then gradually increase.

3. Optimize Protein Intake

Goal: 1.2–1.5 grams protein per kilogram body weight per day (≈ 90–110 g for a 150-lb person).

Lean meats, poultry, fish

Eggs and dairy (Greek yogurt, cottage cheese)

Plant proteins: tofu, lentils, beans

Timing: Distribute protein evenly across meals and after workouts.

4. Balance Your Diet

Healthy fats: Olive oil, avocado, nuts, fatty fish

Whole grains: Oats, brown rice, quinoa

Colorful fruits & vegetables: Provide antioxidants and micronutrients

Hydration: Aim for 8–10 cups of water daily unless restricted by your doctor

5. Incorporate Shilajit Safely

Dosage: Follow the manufacturer's instructions—commonly 250–500 mg daily.

Purity: Choose a standardized, lab-tested brand to avoid heavy metals or contaminants.

Timing: Take with meals to aid absorption and reduce stomach upset.

Duration: Evaluate benefits and side effects after 8–12 weeks; reassess with your doctor.

Monitoring Progress and Safety

Track strength gains: Record weights, bands, reps, and any improvements in daily tasks.

Assess body composition: If possible, get periodic muscle‐mass measurements (e.g., DEXA scan).

Watch for side effects: Stomach upset, allergic reactions, or changes in kidney function.

Stay in touch with healthcare providers: Adjust your plan based on lab results or new symptoms.

Lifestyle Habits for Muscle Health

Quality Sleep: Aim for 7–8 hours nightly to optimize hormone balance and recovery.

Stress Management: Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which can break down muscle. Try meditation, breathing exercises, or gentle yoga.

Stay Active Daily: Beyond workouts, walk, garden, or swim to maintain mobility and circulation.

Avoid Excess Alcohol and Smoking: Both can accelerate muscle loss and impair recovery.

When to Seek Professional Input

If you experience any of the following, speak with a healthcare professional promptly:

Unexplained muscle weakness or rapid weight changes

Severe joint or muscle pain interfering with daily life

Persistent fatigue that rest doesn't improve

New or worsening medical conditions

Putting It All Together

Maintaining muscle over age 70 requires a multifaceted approach:

Medical oversight—always involve your doctor before starting supplements like shilajit.

Consistent resistance training to challenge and rebuild muscle fibers.

Adequate, high-quality protein and balanced nutrition.

Safe, purified shilajit as an adjunct—supporting energy, antioxidants, and hormones.

Healthy habits: sleep, stress reduction, daily movement, and hydration.

By blending exercise, nutrition, lifestyle changes, and carefully selected supplements under medical guidance, you can slow or even reverse age-related muscle mass loss. Don't hesitate to discuss any serious or life-threatening concerns with your physician—your health and safety always come first.
