Explanation

Sore nipples can be uncomfortable, especially for breastfeeding mothers. Here are some ways to help ease the pain:

Warm Compresses: Applying a warm compress to the nipples can help reduce pain and promote healing.

Proper Breastfeeding Techniques: Make sure your baby is latching on correctly. A good latch can prevent and alleviate nipple soreness.

Keep Nipples Dry and Clean: After feeding, gently pat your nipples dry and allow them to air out. This helps prevent infections and promotes healing.

Use of Lanolin Cream: Some mothers find relief using lanolin-based creams, which can soothe and protect the skin.

These methods, as reported in the literature, are effective at home remedies for managing nipple soreness.
