# Giving New Life to Old Jeans

Calling all recycling enthusiasts and denim heads: Nudie Jeans, known for their raw denim and hip, downtown vibe, has partnered with ISKO, an international denim manufacturer, to develop their first line of post consumer recycled denim. The Swedish brand gave new life to around 500 pairs of worn out Nudie jeans by combining them with cotton to make jeans that are soft, unique, and comfortable. Recycling never felt so good.

Nudie’s “Post Recycle Dry” denim can be purchased here.
