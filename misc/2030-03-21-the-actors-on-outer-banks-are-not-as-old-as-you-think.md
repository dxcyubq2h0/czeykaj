# The Actors on 'Outer Banks' Are Not As Old As You Think

In Hollywood, it seems like common practice to cast good-looking actors in their 20s (and sometimes 30s) to portray angst-ridden and sexually frustrated teenagers. And Netflix's latest teen drama, Outer Banks, is no exception. The show itself tells the story of two drastically different teenage groups (the poor Pogues and the rich Kooks) as they search for buried treasure hidden somewhere in their quaint, idyllic beach town in North Carolina.

The cast plays typical TV adolescents; they kick back a beer—or three—and fall in love. But as it turns out, the actors probably don't need a fake ID to get into a bar. Most of them are actually in their 20s. Here's a look at the cast and their ages, compared to those of their characters.
