What is IgG4-Related Skin Disease?

Immunoglobulin G4-related disease is linked to unregulated immune system responses and can affect the skin. It can appear in various ways on the skin, and other organs may also be affected by IgG4-RD. It affects men more than women and is more common in older adults than in young people.

Typical Symptoms of IgG4-Related Skin Disease

Multiple areas of skin redness or red spots

Black/blue/brown spots or moles, on the skin and lining of the mouth

Dark spots on skin

Skin problems

Redness, bumps, or irritation on the skin

Diagnostic Questions for IgG4-Related Skin Disease

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have multiple red areas or spots on your skin?

Are there black, blue, or brown spots on your skin? (Including moles)

Do you have darkened skin?

Do you have any skin problems

Have you noticed any redness, bumps, or irritation on your skin?
