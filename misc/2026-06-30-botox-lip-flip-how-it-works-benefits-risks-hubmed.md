# Botox Lip Flip: How It Works, Benefits & Risks | Hubmed

With the rise and expansion of aesthetic procedures, there has been a significant increase in the popularity of non-invasive procedures and treatments. This is how the Botox lip flip has come up as a mild alternative to traditional lip fillers, offering a subtler enhancement to the upper lip's appearance. By injecting small amounts of botulinum toxin (Botox) into the orbicularis oris muscle, the upper lip relaxes and rolls outward slightly, creating the illusion of fuller lips without adding volume. This technique requires precision and expertise to achieve natural-looking results while avoiding potential complications.

Seeing as this is an off-label use of Botox, the lip flip is not FDA-approved. Still, it’s widely practiced by experienced medical professionals and very sought-after by clients. Understanding its benefits, risks, and best practices is essential for practitioners integrating this procedure into their aesthetic services. In this article, we explore the science behind the Botox lip flip, its advantages, potential risks, best injection techniques, and how it compares to traditional lip fillers.

What is a Botox Lip Flip and how does it work?

A Botox lip flip is a cosmetic procedure that enhances the upper lip's appearance by strategically injecting Botox into the muscles around the mouth. Unlike dermal fillers that add volume, Botox relaxes the muscles, causing the upper lip to roll slightly outward. This creates the effect of a fuller, more defined lip without increasing its actual size.

Injection Sites: Botox is typically injected into the orbicularis oris muscle, which controls lip movement.

Muscle Relaxation: The targeted muscles relax, reducing tension and allowing the upper lip to lift slightly.

Subtle Enhancement: The result is a natural-looking lip enhancement, ideal for patients who want a mild improvement rather than dramatic volume.

How to Do a Lip Flip with Botox

A skilled injector will administer 4 to 10 units of Botox into precise points above the upper lip. The key Botox injection points for a lip flip typically include:

Two injections at the corners of the upper lip (Cupid’s bow area)

Two injections near the edges of the upper lip to prevent excessive downward movement

The procedure takes only a few minutes, with minimal downtime.

Benefits of a Botox Lip Flip

The usage of Botox for lip flip procedure instead of dermal fillers results in subtle enhancement which appears to be more natural. The shape of the upper lip becomes more pronounced without actually adding any volume. In addition to subtlety, patients prefer this procedure as it’s not only quick, but also minimally invasive – it takes less than 15 minutes, and results last for months. When doing lip flip with Botox, patients typically have to put aside smaller amount of money, as the procedure costs usually range from $50 to $200 per session. Finally, because the results are temporary, this procedure is perfect for patients who want to test lip enhancement before committing to fillers.

Who Should Avoid a Botox Lip Flip?

If patients have thin lips and would like to add noticeable volume, they should opt for classic lip fillers instead. Also, patients who have muscle weakness in the lip area will not be suitable for this procedure, as Botox will further relax the muscles. People who require strong lip function should not undergo this procedure as it will impact their careers and daily lives, so musicians, teachers, public speakers, etc. should avoid it. Lastly, if patients are looking for long-lasting results, they should go with another kind of treatment.

How Long Does a Botox Lip Flip Last?

The first results of Botox lip flip typically start appearing within 3-5 days and peak at 10-14 days after injection. The results last 6 to 8 weeks and gradually fade as the muscle function returns since the effects of Botox in this area fade faster than in other facial regions. Regular maintenance treatments are required to sustain the results.

Potential Risks and Downsides of a Botox Lip Flip

While the Botox lip flip is a safe and effective procedure, patients should be aware of possible risks and downsides. Common side effects include temporary difficulty with drinking from a straw, whistling, or pronouncing certain words due to swelling, bruising or tenderness at the injection sites.

The results are rather subtle, which means that patients who want more volume might be disappointed by the outcome. In addition to this, the effects of Botox lip flip tend to wear off rather quickly, which would require frequent touch-ups. Finally, if not injected correctly, there is a risk of asymmetry or Botox migration, which could affect nearby muscles.

FAQs About the Botox Lip Flip

Does a lip flip change your smile?

Yes, a lip flip can slightly alter the smile by limiting upper lip movement. This effect is temporary and wears off as Botox metabolizes.

Does a lip flip give you duck lips?

No, a Botox lip flip does not add volume, so it won’t create a “duck lip” appearance. Instead, it subtly enhances the natural lip shape.

How painful is a lip flip?

Most patients report minimal discomfort, as the injections are quick and performed with fine needles.

How much Botox is needed for a lip flip?

Most treatments require 4 to 10 units of Botox, with the average being 6 units.

How long until I see results?

Initial results appear in 3 to 5 days, with full effects developing around 10 to 14 days after treatment.

What do Botox lip flips look like?

Botox lip flip before and after photos typically show a slightly lifted, more defined upper lip, creating a fuller appearance without added volume.

How much does a Botox lip flip cost?

Costs range from $50-$200, depending on location and provider expertise.
