# Chris Hemsworth Is Looking More Shredded Than Ever in New Shirtless Workout Selfie

Never let it be said that Chris Hemsworth rests on his laurels. In addition to continuing to play the role of Thor in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, he has launched another burgeoning action franchise over on Netflix with Extraction—he's currently filming the sequel—and is set to star in the biopic of wrestling legend Hulk Hogan.

The Australian actor prepared for each of these physically demanding projects by training even harder than usual, packing on an impressive amount of muscle over the last year. He has also pushed himself far out of his comfort zones, both physically and mentally, for Limitless, an upcoming National Geographic documentary series which explores the different methods that people all over the world use to live healthier, longer lives.

Hemsworth has documented his body transformation on Instagram, flexing his increasingly huge biceps in thirst traps and sharing video clip outtakes from his intense workouts with his stunt double Bobby Holland Hanton, which have included sled pulls, boxing drills, Bosu ball squats, and bodyweight routines (as well as "lumberjack" training). His Thor: Love and Thunder and Extraction 2 training plan was all programmed by trainer Luke Zocchi, who is also one of the coaches that put together the workouts and meal plans for Hemsworth's health and wellness app Centr.

His latest progress pic, shot by photographer Cristian Prieto, highlights his hulking shoulders and jacked arm and chest gains as he gets a pump on the cable machine. "Finishing the week strong with some well deserved Iron Punishment!!" He wrote in the caption.
