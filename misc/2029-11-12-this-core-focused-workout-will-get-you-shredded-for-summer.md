# This Core-Focused Workout Will Get You Shredded for Summer

MAKING THE MOST of your summer has different meanings foreveryone. Maybe you’re a beach vacation bum and you live for the shore, or you have a regular park hangout planned with your buddies once the weather hits that perfect point to hit the court for hoops or pickleball. The season offers so many opportunities—so no matter what type of guy you are, you should take your workouts seriously so you can look, feel, and move your absolute best.

The new Summer Shred workout plan for Men's Health MVP Premium members gives you the tools to do just that. Trainer Korey Rowe, ACE designed the program to challenge you with hard-hitting, smart workouts that will push you to your limits in the moment while setting you up for success outside the gym. “The thing I’ve always loved about fitness is there’s a direct correlation between the work you put in and the outcome you receive,” says Rowe. Translation: Put your all into these workouts and there will be a major payoff.
