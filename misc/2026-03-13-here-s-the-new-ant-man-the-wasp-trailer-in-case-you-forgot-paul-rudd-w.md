# Here's the New 'Ant-Man & the Wasp' Trailer, in Case You Forgot Paul Rudd Was Fit

Prepare yourself for another fun-sized trip to the Quantum Realm, because Marvel just dropped the latest look at Ant-Man & the Wasp.

Set before the (emotionally devastating) events of Avengers: Infinity War, director Peyton Reed's superhero flick picks up with a house-arrested Scott Lang (Paul Rudd) teaming up with the equally inconsistently-sized Hope Van Dyne (Evangeline Lilly) to take on a new Marvel villain, Ghost.

Still can't buy Paul Rudd as an ass-kicker?

Then you should know that the Anchorman star has been putting in work since his 2013 casting to stand beside the Dave Bautistas and assorted Chrises of the MCU. Rudd looked to fellow funnyman-turned-fighter Chris Pratt for advice on dropping pounds and bulking up for the role.

“I basically didn’t eat anything for about a year,” Rudd semi-joked to Variety. “I took the Chris Pratt approach to training for an action movie. Eliminate anything fun for a year and then you can play a hero.”

The Ant-Man star also told People he did away with alcohol for an entire year, focusing instead on a no-carb diet and a 24/7 dedication to fitness. “My day was centered around fitness and health, and that was kind of a first,” he said.
