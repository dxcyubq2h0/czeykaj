The curse goes all the way back to 1666, so the kids have a lot of work ahead of them. And that’s not even counting the homework they’re probably missing.

The first iteration introduced us to Heather Watkins (Maya Hawke of Stranger Things), who immediately is murdered inexplicably by her friend Ryan. The killing ignites the interest of other teens in Shadyside, Ohio, who suspect a witch’s curse before her execution 300 years ago is the catalyst, plus the reason why the town is known across the US as a dangerous place to live.

Fear Street: 1978 continues the story, while focusing on campers and counselors at Camp Nightwing, who have to band together to survive horrors originating from the town’s history. The third film, Fear Street: 1666, will be the definitive conclusion of the terrifying, time-traveling trilogy.
