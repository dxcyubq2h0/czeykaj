# The Best Place for a Nap

[Best Place](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Want to sleep like a baby during your next power nap? Turns out there’s a reason we cradle infants to sleep and love snoozing in hammocks: Napping on a slowly swinging bed really does get us to sleep faster, reveals a study released in this month's Current Biology.

In the study, 12 male subjects ages 22 to 38 took two 45-minute afternoon naps—one on a stationary bed and one on a bed put in rocking motion to simulate a hammock. While they slept, researchers monitored the participants’ brain activities.

Each of the subjects fell asleep more quickly when the bed was swaying. Plus, eight of them said the rocking condition was more enjoyable than the stationary bed.

Rocking also helped the volunteers sleep more deeply. The movement increased the length of N2 sleep, which is a form of deep sleep that occurs when you get a good night’s sleep. The quality of their N2 sleep improved as well.

So what’s the ideal nap? It’s in a hammock, it’s 10 minutes long, and it’s in the afternoon. People who napped for 10 minutes had better concentration and memory following their snooze than people who napped for other lengths, Australian researchers found. And when adults napped between 2 and 4 p.m., they performed better on tests and had no problems falling asleep at night, according to a study reported about in the Men’s Health article How to sleep right, tonight.
