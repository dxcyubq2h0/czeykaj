What is GLP‑1?

GLP‑1 (Glucagon-Like Peptide 1) is a naturally occurring hormone in the body that plays a key role in regulating your appetite, food intake, and blood sugar levels. Released by the intestines after eating, GLP‑1 signals your brain to reduce hunger, slow down gastric emptying, and improve insulin sensitivity. This natural appetite suppression is one of the reasons why GLP‑1 weight loss medications have become a go-to treatment for those struggling with weight management.

How Do GLP‑1 Medications Work for Weight Loss?

In recent years, GLP‑1 agonists have been developed to mimic the effects of the natural hormone in your body. These medications work by targeting several key factors involved in weight gain and loss, including:

Slower Gastric Emptying: By slowing the rate at which food leaves your stomach, GLP‑1 medications prolong the feeling of fullness. This helps to keep you satisfied after meals and reduces the urge to snack between meals.

Improved Insulin Sensitivity: GLP‑1 agonists also enhance the body’s ability to use insulin, a hormone that helps regulate blood sugar levels. Better insulin sensitivity means your body can store and burn fat more efficiently, supporting weight loss and improving overall health.

As with any medical treatment, safety is a key consideration. GLP‑1 weight loss medications have been thoroughly tested in clinical trials and are generally well-tolerated.
