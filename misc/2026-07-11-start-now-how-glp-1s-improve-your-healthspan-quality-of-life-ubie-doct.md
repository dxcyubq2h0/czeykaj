What the Research Says

LEADER (liraglutide) and SUSTAIN-6 (semaglutide) showed significant drops in heart attack and stroke rates.

STEP studies highlight dramatic weight loss and metabolic improvements over 68–104 weeks.

Animal studies suggest GLP-1s lower markers of chronic inflammation, a known driver of aging.

Ongoing human trials are examining effects on biomarkers like C-reactive protein (CRP) and telomere length.

While long-term data (5+ years) in healthy individuals are still emerging, the consistency of benefits across diverse patient groups is encouraging for those focused on long term healthspan.

Monitoring and Safety

GLP-1 receptor agonists are generally well tolerated, but you should be aware of common side effects and safety measures:

Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea or constipation (usually mild and transient)

Early satiety (may require dietary adjustments)

Pancreatitis (seek medical help for severe abdominal pain)

Gallbladder issues (gallstones, cholecystitis)

Rare risk of thyroid C-cell tumors in animal studies (human relevance unclear)
