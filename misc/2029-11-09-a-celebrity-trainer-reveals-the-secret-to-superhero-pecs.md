THE MOST ICONIC moment in pectoral cinematic history occurs early in 2011’s Captain America: The First Avenger. When a shirtless Chris Evans steps out of the pod that transforms his character, Steve Rogers, into a superhero, Hayley Atwell’s Peggy Carter taps his rippling, hairless chest.

[Superhero Pecs](https://nootropic-atlas.escortskhi.com/blog/3631340158.html)

Not that this is the only time a superhero chest has drawn attention. From Evans’s Cap to Chris Hemsworth’s Thor to Ryan Reynolds’s Deadpool, firm, sculpted pecs are at the center of many superhero-movie moments. Expect that again later this year when Reynolds reprises his role as Wade Wilson in Deadpool 3. “A well-defined, rounded chest—it’s what’s hitting you head-on,” says Don Saladino, Reynolds’s longtime trainer.

[Reveals the Secret](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

Reynolds spent much of last year working with Saladino to pack muscle onto his chest. But at age 47, he’s doing it with new tactics. The barbell bench press is the gold standard for chest development, but very often it can lead to shoulder issues. Saladino makes several adjustments to guard against that. “The name of the game for Ryan is resiliency,” the trainer says. That’s why he adds these three bench tweaks to Reynolds’s training.

[Trainer Reveals the](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/6032186522.html)

Train to Tempo

Saladino often pushes Reynolds to bench press slowly, forcing him to lower for three full seconds and sometimes to press up for that long, too. “It allows us to load less weight,” the trainer says,“which is sometimes a bit easier on his joints.” Try it, doing 3 sets of 8 to 10 reps of dumbbell presses.

Elbows In!

When Reynolds settles in for bench presses, Saladino zones in on elbow position. Chest-press fanatics often flare their elbows as they bench. Not Reynolds, who aims to keep his at a 45 degree angle relative to his torso. “We’re trying to get a little bit more into packing those elbows,” Saladino says. This protects your shoulders from injury.

Begin with Pushups

Before Reynolds does any of that, he starts with a few sets of a pressing exercise, such as pushups. Saladino loves the move as a way of priming Reynolds’s joints for the heavier weights to come. “I like to use it as an activation,” he says. “It allows the blood to get into the muscle and allows the joint to get warm.” Lead your chest workouts with 3 sets of 10 to 12 pushup reps.
