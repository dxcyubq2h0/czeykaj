GLP-1 medications, including semaglutide, can occasionally cause muscle weakness. In rare cases, this weakness may be linked to serious conditions such as rhabdomyolysis, a disorder involving muscle breakdown. On the other hand, emerging research suggests GLP-1 drugs may also offer benefits for certain muscle-related conditions, making their effects on muscle health complex and worth monitoring.

Explanation

GLP-1 drugs, such as semaglutide, are used to manage conditions like diabetes but can sometimes affect muscles. Muscle Weakness Signs: Muscle weakness from these drugs can sometimes be linked to a condition called rhabdomyolysis, which is when muscles break down and cause pain and weakness. This was discussed in one of the studies where semaglutide was associated with this condition. Potential Benefits: Interestingly, another study found that semaglutide might help reverse some chronic muscle problems, suggesting it could have beneficial effects on muscle function in certain situations. Important Note: While the potential for muscle problems exists, the effects can vary, and semaglutide might not cause issues for everyone. If you experience muscle pain or weakness while on these medications, you can check your symptoms to better understand what might be happening, though it's important to talk to a healthcare provider for proper evaluation.
