How biomarker testing services work

A biomarker testing service offers blood-based testing, usually on a membership, that measures your biomarkers at set intervals. Biomarkers are measurable signals in your blood, things like cholesterol, blood sugar, and hormones.

Some platforms stop at data and a report. Others turn those results into a plan you can follow. Two questions separate them: how much do they measure, and what do they help you do next?

Superpower and InsideTracker answer both differently.

Cost and value compared

Superpower is $199 a year, one flat membership. InsideTracker is à-la-carte, so a fair annual comparison adds its $149 membership to one Ultimate test at the $340 member rate, roughly $489 a year. A single one-off Ultimate, bought without a membership, costs more, though the current store funnels new buyers toward the ~$489 membership bundle. InsideTracker also sells multi-test bundles up front, like membership plus two Ultimate tests for about $761, which trims the cost of the second test versus buying a retest later at the member rate.

Optional add-ons cost extra — a DNA cheek swab (38 wellness traits) is about $249, InnerAge about $99, and an estradiol-plus-progesterone hormone add-on about $89. Re-testing follows the same pattern: an InsideTracker retest is about $340 at the member rate, while Superpower's full 100+ biomarker follow-up is a $179 add-on.

Both are HSA/FSA eligible, so pre-tax dollars can cover either. The value question is what you want bundled, and at what yearly cost.

Our take

InsideTracker is genuinely powerful for athletes and optimizers: optional DNA insights, the InnerAge biological-age product, and deep wearable and external-lab integration.

But it's built around a narrower core panel at a premium price. Superpower gives you 100+ health metrics in one draw, a broader whole-body baseline, plus a personalized protocol, on-demand care, and a supplement and prescription marketplace, all for $199 a year, well under InsideTracker's yearly cost.

A polished dashboard is only as good as what you do with it. Superpower pairs its wider panel with the protocol, care, and marketplace to act on it, and 60% of members say it revealed something a doctor had overlooked. Optimize from the whole read, not a narrow one.

Frequently asked questions

Does Superpower cost less than InsideTracker?

Yes. Superpower is $199 a year for an all-in-one membership, while InsideTracker runs about $489 a year once you add its $149 membership and an Ultimate test. Superpower's price also bundles a protocol, supplements, and prescription access.

How many biomarkers does InsideTracker test?

InsideTracker's Ultimate plan measures 54 blood biomarkers. Superpower runs 100+ health metrics from a single blood draw, combining direct lab measurements with calculated ratios.

Does Superpower test more than InsideTracker?

They count on different bases. Superpower's 100+ health metrics span direct lab measurements plus calculated ratios, all from one blood draw. InsideTracker's Ultimate panel is 54 direct blood biomarkers, so Superpower measures more distinct metrics from a single draw. InsideTracker's DNA (38 wellness traits) and hormone add-ons are separate paid purchases, not part of the blood panel.

Does InsideTracker prescribe medication?

No. InsideTracker provides nutrition and lifestyle recommendations only and does not prescribe. Superpower offers access to prescription medications in the marketplace (medications priced separately).

Is InsideTracker better than Superpower?

It depends on what you want. InsideTracker is strong for athletes and optimizers who want DNA insights, an InnerAge biological-age read, and deep wearable integration. For most people who want to act on their results, Superpower bundles a protocol, on-demand care, and prescriptions at a lower yearly cost.

Can I use an HSA/FSA for Superpower?

Yes. Superpower accepts HSA and FSA payment for its annual membership.

Is Superpower worth it?

For most people, yes. Superpower says 85% of members rated their action plan more useful, even life-changing, than a yearly checkup. You're paying for the plan and care that turn optimization data into changes, not just another dashboard.
